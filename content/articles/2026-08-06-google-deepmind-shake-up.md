Google announced its biggest AI leadership change since the DeepMind merger on August 5: Demis Hassabis steps down as Google DeepMind CEO to become [Chair of DeepMind and Chief Scientist of Alphabet](https://blog.google/company-news/inside-google/message-ceo/next-chapter-ai-momentum/), CTO Koray Kavukcuoglu takes day-to-day charge as SVP reporting to Sundar Pichai, and Jeff Dean — Google's chief scientist and employee number ~30 — leaves after 27 years with three other senior researchers to found a startup called Discovery Loop. Here is what actually changed, the 2026 exit pattern behind it, and what it means for Google's very large bets on India.

## What Google actually announced

The announcement came as a joint post from Pichai and Hassabis titled ["The next chapter of our AI momentum."](https://blog.google/company-news/inside-google/message-ceo/next-chapter-ai-momentum/) Hassabis hands over operational control of the unit he has run since DeepMind and Google Brain [merged in April 2023](https://www.cnbc.com/2023/04/20/alphabet-merges-ai-focused-groups-deepmind-and-google-research.html), moves to a strategic Chair role, and adds a newly created Alphabet-wide Chief Scientist title. "I've been working towards AGI my whole life and now, like many of you, I feel it is close at hand," he wrote, adding that DeepMind has "become the AI engine room of Google."

Notably, nobody inherits the CEO title. Pichai's wording is precise: "Koray, the current Chief Technology Officer of GDM and our Chief AI Architect, will step up as SVP of Google DeepMind, reporting to me." Kavukcuoglu — a 13-year DeepMind veteran behind WaveNet and DQN, [named Google's first Chief AI Architect in June 2025](https://www.semafor.com/article/06/11/2025/google-names-new-chief-ai-architect-to-advance-developments) — now runs Gemini model development, frontier research, the Gemini app and developer platforms. Hassabis's reassurance: "The Gemini models are in good hands with Koray and the leads." No effective date appears anywhere in the post.

Hassabis is not leaving Alphabet. He continues as CEO of [Isomorphic Labs](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/isomorphic-labs-secures-2-1-billion-funding-to-scale-its-ai-drug-design-engine-302769674.html), the drug-discovery spin-off that raised $2.1 billion in May 2026, and he keeps advising on AGI strategy. The scale of what Kavukcuoglu inherits is in the same post: Pichai says the [Gemini app has passed 950 million monthly users and Gemma models 900 million downloads](https://blog.google/company-news/inside-google/message-ceo/next-chapter-ai-momentum/).

## Discovery Loop: four architects walk out one door

The same post confirms Jeff Dean's departure — and undersells it. Google named only Dean, but [reporters quickly established](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/05/jeff-dean-and-other-top-ai-researchers-are-leaving-google-to-launch-their-own-startup/) that three more of Google's most cited researchers are leaving with him to co-found Discovery Loop, a Palo Alto public benefit corporation whose stated mission is to [automate machine learning, science and engineering](https://www.discoveryloop.com/) to accelerate discovery.

| Founder | Tenure and role | Best known for |
|---|---|---|
| Jeff Dean | 27 years — joined 1999, roughly employee #30, [per GeekWire](https://www.geekwire.com/2026/the-startup-idea-that-convinced-a-uw-computer-science-legend-to-leave-google-after-27-years/) | MapReduce, Bigtable, Spanner, TensorFlow, TPUs; Chief Scientist since 2023 |
| Sanjay Ghemawat | Senior Fellow, [per TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/05/jeff-dean-and-other-top-ai-researchers-are-leaving-google-to-launch-their-own-startup/) | Dean's legendary coding partner on GFS, MapReduce, Bigtable, Spanner |
| Oriol Vinyals | ~13 years — VP of Research, DeepMind | seq2seq, AlphaStar; a Gemini technical co-lead |
| Quoc Le | ~15 years — Google Fellow | Google Brain co-founder, AutoML, chain-of-thought prompting |

The financing is as notable as the roster. Google itself is a founding investor and will be Discovery Loop's cloud partner — [for at least the first year, per GeekWire](https://www.geekwire.com/2026/the-startup-idea-that-convinced-a-uw-computer-science-legend-to-leave-google-after-27-years/) — with a seed round [co-led by Radical Ventures and Khosla Ventures, joined by Kleiner Perkins, Lightspeed and Doerr Capital](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/05/jeff-dean-and-other-top-ai-researchers-are-leaving-google-to-launch-their-own-startup/). No amount was disclosed. Dean's pitch, [as he told GeekWire](https://www.geekwire.com/2026/the-startup-idea-that-convinced-a-uw-computer-science-legend-to-leave-google-after-27-years/): "Imagine a future where a handful of people can conduct scientific research and engineering tasks much more rapidly, and with higher quality, than massive teams of scientists and engineers do today."

## The 2026 exodus, in one table

August 5 is the fourth major DeepMind talent story this year, and the pattern is the story.

| When (2026) | Who left | Where they went |
|---|---|---|
| Early 2026 | David Silver, AlphaGo/AlphaZero architect | Founded Ineffable Intelligence; [reported $1B+ seed led by Sequoia](https://www.techfundingnews.com/david-silver-deepmind-1b-ineffable-intelligence/) |
| June 18 | Noam Shazeer, Gemini co-lead | [To OpenAI](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/18/google-gemini-co-lead-noam-shazeer-leaves-for-openai.html) — two years after Google's [reported $2.7B Character.AI deal](https://techcrunch.com/2024/08/02/character-ai-ceo-noam-shazeer-returns-to-google) brought him back |
| June 20 | John Jumper, AlphaFold lead and Nobel laureate | [To Anthropic](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/20/nobel-laureate-john-jumper-is-leaving-deepmind-for-rival-anthropic/) |
| Late July | AlphaFold's dedicated team | [FT-sourced reports say it was broken up](https://www.engadget.com/2225849/google-shuts-down-alphafold/) across Gemini work, Isomorphic and exits; Google has not confirmed |
| Aug 5 | Dean, Ghemawat, Vinyals, Le | [Discovery Loop](https://www.discoveryloop.com/) — with Google as founding investor |

Two clarifications the aggregated coverage keeps blurring. First, the "Google dismantled the Nobel-winning AlphaFold team" claim circulating online traces to [late-July reports](https://www.engadget.com/2225849/google-shuts-down-alphafold/), not to this week's announcement, which never mentions AlphaFold; Google's only on-record response has been that "many of the researchers behind AlphaFold continue to drive scientific and technological advances across Google, Google DeepMind, and Isomorphic Labs" — a statement about people, not about the team's structure. Second, Hassabis and Jumper [shared half of the 2024 Nobel Prize in Chemistry](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/chemistry/2024/summary/) for AlphaFold; only Jumper has left. As recently as June, Hassabis was publicly dismissive of exit talk, telling Semafor that Google has ["by far the biggest and broadest research bench of any of the labs"](https://www.semafor.com/article/06/23/2026/deepmind-chief-demis-hassabis-says-googles-still-winning-ai-talent).

## What it means

The cleanest way to read Hassabis's move is against the only two precedents that fit. In January 2000, Bill Gates [handed Microsoft's CEO job to Steve Ballmer and made himself Chief Software Architect](https://www.historylink.org/file/10426). In December 2019, Larry Page and Sergey Brin [stepped back from Alphabet in favour of Sundar Pichai](https://blog.google/alphabet/letter-from-larry-and-sergey/), staying on as board members. Founder keeps the technology title and the long-term questions; an operator takes delivery. The twist here is that Kavukcuoglu does not get the CEO title at all — he is an SVP reporting to Pichai. DeepMind, acquired in 2014 and already [folded into Google proper in 2023](https://www.cnbc.com/2023/04/20/alphabet-merges-ai-focused-groups-deepmind-and-google-research.html), now looks less like a research house with a chief executive and more like a Google product division. That is the org-chart admission that Gemini is Google's core product now.

The market read it as loss, then thought again. Alphabet [fell as much as 5.4% intraday](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/alphabet-shares-fall-5-ai-164713908.html) on the news, but [closed down 3.53%](https://www.tradingkey.com/news/market-movers/262078737-market-movers-googl-20260805) on a day when the same recap attributed the slide mainly to antitrust-remedy reports and AI capex worries — so treat "the stock fell 5% because of Jeff Dean" as directionally true at best. The sharper signal is competitive: [Reuters tied the reshuffle's timing](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/google-shakes-up-ai-leadership-as-deepmind-chief-shifts-role-160227886.html) to a Gemini 4 that reportedly missed a June target, and the [Hacker News discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49184746) kept returning to one theme: DeepMind's equity upside cannot match what OpenAI or Anthropic paper gains — or a Google-funded startup of your own — can offer. When the cheap end of the market is being squeezed by [DeepSeek's $0.28-per-million-token V4 Flash](/articles/deepseek-v4-flash-0731/) and [Alibaba's Qwen3.8-Max](/articles/qwen3-8-max-what-alibaba-launched/), and the premium end by [OpenAI's 80% price cut on GPT-5.6 Luna](/articles/gpt-5-6-luna-price-cut/), losing four architects in a day is a cost Google chose to absorb gracefully — even invest in — rather than fight.

## The India angle

No outlet covering the shake-up asked the India question, which is strange, because India is where Google's AI money is currently going. Its biggest announced investment in India is the [$15 billion AI hub in Visakhapatnam](https://blog.google/intl/en-in/company-news/our-first-ai-hub-in-india-powered-by-a-15-billion-investment/) — a gigawatt-scale data-centre campus with a new subsea cable gateway, built with AdaniConneX and Airtel, targeted for construction from early 2026. On the demand side, the [Reliance Jio deal](https://blog.google/intl/en-in/company-news/partnering-with-reliance-to-bring-the-best-of-google-ai-to-more-people-across-india/) gives [500 million Jio users 18 months of Google AI Pro free](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/10/31/google-gemini-ai-free-500-million-jio-relince-users-india-.html) — a bundle Google values at about ₹35,100 — running until October 29, 2026. Both bets were championed while Hassabis ran DeepMind and Pichai personally fronted the India pitch.

The research side is the part to watch. DeepMind lists [Bangalore among its ten offices worldwide](https://deepmind.google/careers/), and its India team works on efficient models, continual learning and multilingual AI — the mandate Hassabis himself described in February when he told the India AI Impact Summit that [India will be "a powerhouse for AI across the globe."](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/india-ai-impact-summit-2026-demis-hassabis-says-india-set-to-be-powerhouse-in-ai-as-google-deepmind-expands-research-partnerships-516934-2026-02-19) Days before the reshuffle, DeepMind's India leadership was showcasing [AIIMS researchers using MedGemma, IISc's Project Vaani speech dataset, and 150,000-plus Indian researchers on the AlphaFold database](https://www.business-standard.com/technology/artificial-intelligence/indians-involved-in-solving-world-s-toughest-problems-google-deepmind-126080200459_1.html). Whether Kavukcuoglu — who has no public India statement on record — protects that multilingual research mandate, or narrows Bengaluru toward Gemini product work, is now a genuinely open question for Indian AI research.

There is also a quiet Indian thread inside the departure itself: Sanjay Ghemawat, the systems legend leaving with Dean, [grew up in Kota, Rajasthan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanjay_Ghemawat). But none of the five principals in this reshuffle is India-based — India's DeepMind leadership remains regional. For a market Google keeps calling central, the decisions that shape it are still made a dozen time zones away.

## What to watch

Watch for an effective date and any follow-on exits — [the coverage that confirmed the four departures](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/05/jeff-dean-and-other-top-ai-researchers-are-leaving-google-to-launch-their-own-startup/) is barely a day old, and transitions like this often trigger a second wave. Watch whether Discovery Loop discloses its round size, and whether Gemini 4 ships this year under Kavukcuoglu. For India: whether the Jio free tier is renewed past October 29, whether Vizag construction starts on schedule, and whether DeepMind Bengaluru's multilingual mandate survives the new org chart. Across [our Big Tech coverage](/topics/big-tech/), this is the one to track: the company that assembled the deepest AI research bench in the world is now betting it can keep the engine running while its architects build elsewhere — partly on Google's own money.
