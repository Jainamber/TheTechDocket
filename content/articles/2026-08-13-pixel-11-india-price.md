Google's Pixel 11 series is official, and India gets it on day one: the **Pixel 11 at ₹89,999**, the **Pixel 11 Pro at ₹1,19,999**, the **Pro XL at ₹1,34,999** and the **Pixel 11 Pro Fold at ₹1,86,999**, with [preorders open now and open sale from August 20](https://www.fonearena.com/blog/489452/google-pixel-11-pixel-11-pro-pixel-11-pro-xl-price-india-specifications.html). Every slab phone costs ₹10,000 more than its Pixel 10 equivalent — and the catch hiding under the price is that Google simultaneously cut the free Google AI Pro bundle to six months on the Pro models and removed it entirely from the base Pixel 11.

## Pixel 11 prices in India: the full list

Google announced four phones at its Made by Google event in New York on August 12 — [Pixel 11, Pixel 11 Pro, Pixel 11 Pro XL and Pixel 11 Pro Fold](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/pixel/made-by-google-2026/) — plus the Pixel Watch 5 and a new AirTag-style tracker called Pixel Tag. The India prices [leaked hours before the event and turned out exactly right](https://gadgets.beebom.com/news/india-pricing-for-google-pixel-11-11-pro-11-pro-xl-11-pro-fold-official), confirmed at launch by [FoneArena's India pricing rundown](https://www.fonearena.com/blog/489452/google-pixel-11-pixel-11-pro-pixel-11-pro-xl-price-india-specifications.html). US prices come from [Google's own announcement](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/pixel/pixel-11-features/).

| Model | India price | US price | Pixel 10 series India launch price | Change |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pixel 11 (12GB/256GB) | ₹89,999 | $899 | ₹79,999 | +₹10,000 (+12.5%) |
| Pixel 11 Pro (256GB) | ₹1,19,999 | $1,099 | ₹1,09,999 | +₹10,000 (+9.1%) |
| Pixel 11 Pro XL (256GB) | ₹1,34,999 | $1,299 | ₹1,24,999 | +₹10,000 (+8.0%) |
| Pixel 11 Pro Fold (512GB) | ₹1,86,999 | $1,899 (256GB) | ₹1,72,999 (256GB) | +₹14,000, storage doubled |

The Pixel 10 comparison prices are the official 2025 India launch figures for the [Pixel 10](https://www.republicworld.com/tech/google-pixel-10-series-launches-in-india-price-starts-at-rs-79999-tensor-g5-brings-major-ai-boost), the [Pixel 10 Pro and Pro XL](https://www.thehansindia.com/technology/tech-news/google-launches-pixel-10-pro-and-pixel-10-pro-xl-in-india-price-specs-and-key-features-999079) and the [Pixel 10 Pro Fold](https://www.gizmochina.com/2025/10/09/google-pixel-10-pro-fold-now-on-sale-in-india-price-specs-what-you-get/). Note two structural changes: the [128GB storage tier is gone across the lineup](https://www.gsmarena.com/here_is_how_much_the_new_pixel_11_phones_cost-news-74144.php), so 256GB is the new floor, and India's Fold comes in a single 512GB Olive configuration — which makes its ₹14,000 jump the softest hike of the four once you account for the doubled storage.

Preorders opened on August 12 and [retail sale starts August 20](https://www.fonearena.com/blog/489452/google-pixel-11-pixel-11-pro-pixel-11-pro-xl-price-india-specifications.html) through the Google Store India, Flipkart and offline chains including Croma, Reliance Digital and Vijay Sales. Launch offers reported at announcement include a ₹10,000 instant discount on HDFC Bank card EMI, up to 24 months of no-cost EMI and an exchange bonus of up to ₹9,000. The [Pixel Watch 5 starts at ₹42,900 for the 41mm size and ₹45,900 for 45mm](https://www.fonearena.com/blog/489460/google-pixel-watch-5-price-india-features.html), arriving the same day as the phones.

## What's new: Tensor G6, cameras and seven years of updates

The chip is the biggest change. Tensor G6 is [reported to be the first smartphone processor built on TSMC's 2nm-class manufacturing node](https://9to5google.com/2026/07/13/pixel-11-tensor-g6-upgrade-tsmc-2nm/) — a smaller node generally means more transistors in the same space and better power efficiency — though Google itself avoids naming its foundry. What Google does claim: [25% faster web browsing, 15% faster app loading, around 20% better power efficiency, and on-device AI that runs up to 3.5 times faster on a TPU with 50% more compute](https://www.androidauthority.com/pixel-11-tensor-g6-tpu-upgrade-explained-3696999/). A TPU is the dedicated portion of the chip that runs AI models locally, without sending data to a server. Those are vendor numbers, not independent tests — we track how chip claims survive contact with reviewers across our [Chips & Hardware coverage](/topics/hardware/).

The rest of the hardware story is steady iteration. The base Pixel 11 keeps a 6.3-inch display and gains a new 48MP main camera that Google says captures [56% more light than the Pixel 10's, behind a camera bar 40% thinner](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/pixel/pixel-11-features/). The Pro and Pro XL push displays to 3,600 nits of peak brightness with a new anti-scratch coating, and the [Pro Fold gets an 8-inch inner screen, a gearless hinge Google claims is three times more durable, IP68 water resistance and a roughly 10% lighter body](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/pixel/pixel-11-pro-fold/). Every Pixel 11 phone carries [seven years of OS, security and feature updates](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/pixel/pixel-11-features/), matching the policy Google set in 2023.

On software, Google is rebranding its phone AI as **Gemini Intelligence**, pitched less as a chatbot and more as an agent that completes tasks across apps. The standout genuinely new capability is [SL2T, a DeepMind model that transcribes American Sign Language into text in real time](https://deepmind.google/blog/putting-sign-language-ai-into-users-hands/), debuting on the Pixel 11 lineup with more sign languages promised later. There's also Magic Capture, which picks the best-timed shot from hundreds of frames, and Rambler, a Gemini-powered dictation mode in Gboard. Some of it is fenced off: the new HiLight notification LED array [skips the base model entirely](https://www.trustedreviews.com/news/the-pixel-11-gets-gemini-intelligence-but-misses-out-on-a-key-new-feature/), part of a widening gap between base and Pro that Google has been engineering since the [DeepMind leadership shake-up](/articles/google-deepmind-shake-up/) concentrated its consumer AI push.

## The real price tag is the AI subscription

Here is the part of the launch that most price lists skip. Last year, every Pixel 10 Pro shipped with 12 months of Google AI Pro. This year, [the Pro, Pro XL and Pro Fold get 6 months, and the base Pixel 11 gets nothing at all](https://9to5google.com/2026/08/12/google-drops-the-ai-pro-to-6-months-with-pixel-11-pro-ditches-free-plan-for-base-model/) — Google halved the flagship perk and deleted the entry-level one in the same keynote where it raised every price by $100.

After the trial, Google AI Pro renews at [$19.99 a month in the US](https://www.androidpolice.com/google-cut-a-top-pixel-11-pro-perk-in-half-and-your-wallet-will-notice-in-six-months-time/). Keep it for the remaining 18 months of a typical two-year ownership period and that is roughly $360 of subscription stacked on top of a $1,099 phone — a number no launch-day price table prints. AI pricing is volatile in both directions right now; OpenAI [cut its GPT-5.6 Luna API prices by 80%](/articles/gpt-5-6-luna-price-cut/) only two weeks ago, which makes a hardware-bundled subscription an increasingly strange thing to meter.

The sharper point emerges when you look across rivals. Samsung markets its core [Galaxy AI features as free forever](https://www.sammobile.com/news/samsung-confirms-which-galaxy-ai-features-will-remain-free-forever/) — yet its new [Galaxy Z Fold8 buyers also receive a 6-month Google AI Pro trial](https://news.samsung.com/us/samsung-galaxy-z-fold8-ultra-fold8-flip8-foldables-perfected-every-way-of-living) as a headline perk. Apple keeps Apple Intelligence free, but its rebuilt Siri will [run on Google's Gemini models under a multi-year licensing deal](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/01/12/apple-google-ai-siri-gemini.html) announced in January. Follow the money and the three flagship ecosystems of 2026 converge on the same counterparty: whichever phone you buy, the AI meter increasingly runs through Google.

| Ecosystem | AI included free | The Google connection | Ongoing cost after freebies |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pixel 11 Pro | 6 months Google AI Pro (was 12) | Native | $19.99/mo (₹1,950/mo in India) |
| Pixel 11 (base) | No trial this year | Native | Same, from day one |
| Samsung Z Fold8 | Core Galaxy AI "free forever" | 6-month Google AI Pro trial bundled | $19.99/mo for AI Pro tiers |
| iPhone | Apple Intelligence free | Siri rebuilt on licensed Gemini models | Optional ChatGPT Plus at $20/mo |

## The India angle

India gets the launch day-and-date with the US — preorders opened August 12 and sale starts August 20, the same 8-day cadence as last year — but it also gets the hike in a market that is already strained. Counterpoint data shows [India's smartphone shipments fell 10% year-on-year in Q2 2026](https://www.fonearena.com/blog/487652/india-smartphone-shipments-q2-2026-nothing-growth-counterpoint.html), with record memory prices pushing costs up across every brand, and Google's hardware VP Shakil Barkat had [pre-signalled the Pixel increase by pointing at exactly those component costs](https://www.smartprix.com/bytes/google-pixel-11-launches-in-india-heres-everything-new-over-the-pixel-10/). The ₹10,000 question is whether the timing undercuts Google's best-ever India run: Pixel shipments in the ultra-premium band above ₹45,000 [grew 68% year-on-year in Q2 2026, the biggest jump of any brand in that tier](https://www.phonearena.com/news/samsung-nothing-google-surprising-stars-q2-2026-india-smartphone-market-report_id181989) — growth Counterpoint attributed partly to Google *not* raising prices. That cushion is now gone, in a market where [more than one in five phones shipped in 2025 was premium and Apple took its highest-ever value share](https://counterpointresearch.com/en/insights/Premiumization-Gains-Strength-in-India-Smartphone-Market-in-2025-Apple-Records-its-Highest-Ever-Value-Share).

The AI-subscription catch, though, stings least in India. Google AI Pro lists at ₹1,950 a month here, but [Reliance Jio subscribers currently get 18 months of it free](https://www.jio.com/google-gemini-offer/) — a longer free run than any Pixel in the box, including the Pros. An Indian buyer on Jio effectively neutralises the perk Google just halved, while a US buyer has no equivalent. Between that and Gemini's two-year-old support for Hindi and eight other Indian languages, the odd result is that the base Pixel 11 — the model Google stripped of its AI trial — loses less of its value in India than almost anywhere else.

## What to watch

The next four weeks decide how this launch is remembered. Apple's September event is [expected around September 8–9 with the iPhone 18 Pro and, per multiple reports, a foldable iPhone](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/07/30/when-will-apple-announce-iphone-18-pro-event/) — Google deliberately shipped first. Against Samsung, the Pixel 11 Pro Fold at $1,899 sits exactly on the base Galaxy Z Fold8's price with a bigger 8-inch inner display, but it is [heavier at 239g versus 201g and slower to charge at 30W](https://www.engadget.com/2235108/pixel-11-pro-fold-vs-galaxy-z-fold-8/) — a genuine trade rather than a clean win.

Watch the independent benchmarks too. Early leaked test scores suggest Tensor G6's raw CPU gains may be modest despite the cutting-edge manufacturing — but those are unverified pre-release leaks on a different benchmark version than rival chips were tested on, so the honest position is to wait for full reviews after August 20. And watch the hardware race widen: with [OpenAI's Jony Ive-designed device reported for 2027](/articles/openai-jony-ive-device/), this may be the last Pixel generation whose competition is only other phones. One thing Google did not say at launch: whether India-sold Pixel 11 units are assembled in India, where its contract manufacturers have been building earlier Pixels — we will flag it when a confirmation lands.
