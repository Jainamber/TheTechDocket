Google DeepMind has released [Gemini 4 Neon](https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/gemini-4-neon-intelligence-gain), its fourth frontier model in seven weeks, scoring 61 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index and 87.4 on LiveBench 2.0 at $3 per million input tokens and $15 per million output tokens. While Neon cuts task costs by 17% below Gemini 4 Carbon, Google AI Pro subscribers report Antigravity IDE entitlement lockouts and cache-miss quota drain.

## How Gemini 4 Neon Scores Across Frontier Benchmarks

Independent evaluations published through November 22, 2026 show Gemini 4 Neon ranking first on LiveBench 2.0 at 87.4 overall and third on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at 61, trailing only Anthropic's Claude Fable 5.2 and OpenAI's GPT-6.2 Astra per [Artificial Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/gemini-4-neon) and [AIFOD's AI SiteFlow review](https://af.net/realtime/gemini-4-neon-claude-fable-5-2-and-gpt-6-2-divide-2026-ai-benchmarks/).

Rather than a single vendor sweeping every evaluation, the late November 2026 leaderboard divides across three distinct domains, per [AIFOD's analysis of 17 frontier models](https://af.net/realtime/gemini-4-neon-claude-fable-5-2-and-gpt-6-2-divide-2026-ai-benchmarks/). Google's Gemini 4 Neon leads browser automation at 95.6 and research synthesis at 94.5 on LiveBench 2.0, completing runs in 22.3 minutes at an average cost of $2.654 per task. Anthropic's Claude Fable 5.2 leads the LMSYS Chatbot Arena at 1,448 Elo and tops the nine-benchmark Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v5.0.1 at 65 in Max Effort mode ($26.54 per task, 42.7 minutes). OpenAI's GPT-6.2 Astra leads LiveBench 2.0 software engineering with a 91.9 coding score, finishing tasks in 15.1 minutes at $1.941 per run.

Inside Google's own lineup across our [AI Models hub](/topics/ai-models/), Gemini 4 Neon eliminates the generation bottleneck that slowed Gemini 4 Carbon. According to [Artificial Analysis benchmark testing](https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/gemini-4-neon-intelligence-gain), Neon streams 104 to 110 output tokens per second versus Carbon's 57 tokens per second, trimming average Intelligence Index task time by 40% from 36 minutes down to 21 to 22.8 minutes. Even though Neon emits roughly 15% more output tokens per task than Carbon (283 million total output tokens across the suite versus the 65.4 million peer median), its 25% lower standard token pricing ($3/$15 per million tokens versus Carbon's $4/$20 standard rate) cuts average evaluation cost by 17% from $7.76 to $6.44 per task, per the [Artificial Analysis model page](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/gemini-4-neon). Carbon's temporary $2/$10 promotional pricing expires on November 30, 2026.

## Coding Agents, Visual Reasoning, and Verbosity Trade-Offs

Tested inside Google's Antigravity CLI, Gemini 4 Neon (xhigh) scores 59 on the Artificial Analysis Coding Agent Index at $6.86 per task, beating Gemini 4 Carbon (55) and matching Claude Fable 5.2 (58) and Claude Opus 5.5 (58) at less than half their execution cost per [Artificial Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/gemini-4-neon-intelligence-gain).

For engineering teams automating multi-file refactoring and test generation, per-task completion cost matters more than raw token prices—a dynamic we detailed in [the real cost of running AI](/articles/real-cost-of-running-ai/). Running the same Coding Agent Index in Claude Code costs $12.61 per task on Claude Opus 5.5 (max) and $15.47 per task on Claude Fable 5.2 (xhigh), meaning Gemini 4 Neon ($6.86) lowers agentic coding spend by 45.6% against Opus 5.5 and 55.7% against Fable 5.2, per [Artificial Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/gemini-4-neon-intelligence-gain). Neon offers a 1-million-token context window with text, image, audio, and video inputs across four effort settings (`low`, `medium`, `high`, and `xhigh`), where `low` costs $0.75 per task in 3.7 minutes and `high` scores 60 at $3.74 in 14.8 minutes ([Artificial Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/gemini-4-neon)).

Visual reasoning and document presentation show the largest gains over earlier releases like [Gemini 3.6 Flash](/articles/gemini-3-6-flash-what-google-launched/). On `GDP.pdf`, Gemini 4 Neon (high) scores 33.4%, jumping 5 percentage points above Gemini 4 Carbon (high, 28.4%), while reaching 1,700 Elo overall and 1,771 Elo for Presentation Quality on `AA-Briefcase`—45 points ahead of prior leader Claude Sonnet 5.5 (max, 1,726 Elo), per [Artificial Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/gemini-4-neon-intelligence-gain). On Google's Multimodal Knowledge Agent (`MKA`) benchmark, Neon hits a 56% Climb Win-Stay Rate, matching Claude Fable 5.2 (57%). The main trade-off at `xhigh` effort is a 59.30-second time to first token and high verbosity (3.482% output-to-input ratio across 8.12 billion input tokens), making local models like [Inkling 2 Mini](/articles/inkling-2-mini-looped-transformer-laptops/) faster for small inner-loop edits ([Artificial Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/gemini-4-neon)).

## Inside the Antigravity Entitlement Desync and Cache Eviction Bugs

Despite Google advertising Gemini 4 Neon across Antigravity IDE for Pro and Ultra plans, developer threads from November 19 to November 23, 2026 document server-side entitlement lockouts and a prompt-cache eviction bug that burns 58% of weekly quota in three hours, per the [Google AI Developers Forum](https://discuss.ai.google.dev/t/gemini-4-neon-high-cache-misses-on-unchanged-prefix-still-burn-quota-after-nov-21-fix-data-from-nov-22/203824).

Three distinct server-side issues affect developers on Antigravity `v1.5.14`:

1. **Direct-Paid Pro Entitlement Desync:** Paid Google AI Pro subscribers (including 5 TB Google One plans) report that while Gemini 4 Neon, Hydrogen, and Argon work on `gemini.google.com`, the desktop Antigravity IDE grays out all Gemini 4 models as `Unavailable` and restricts sessions to Gemini 3.8 Flash, per [November 22 forum bug reports](https://discuss.ai.google.dev/t/entitlement-desync-gemini-4-argon-hydrogen-neon-unavailable-despite-active-google-ai-pro-subscription/203774). Re-authenticating OAuth and resetting onboarding do not fix the stalled backend `userTier` sync.
2. **Reliance Jio Partner Bundle Exclusion & Third-Party Sunset:** Indian subscribers on the Reliance Jio Google AI Pro bundle found all Gemini 4 models blocked in Antigravity alongside a notice that third-party models—Claude Sonnet 4.6 (Thinking), Claude Opus 4.6 (Thinking), and GPT-OSS 120B (Medium)—will be removed on November 30, 2026, per the [Jio bundle thread](https://discuss.ai.google.dev/t/google-ai-pro-jio-bundle-all-gemini-4-models-including-hydrogen-neon-marked-unavailable-in-antigravity/202675). On November 20, 2026, Google support confirmed that the Jio bundle only grants Pro's 5-hour refreshing quota on Gemini 3.8 Flash and excludes Gemini 4 unless users supply a paid `GEMINI_API_KEY` in Antigravity CLI (`v1.5.13+`).
3. **Unchanged-Prefix Cache Collapse Above 250K Context:** SQLite logs from `~/.gemini/antigravity/conversations/*.db` on November 22, 2026—after Google's November 21 cache patch—reveal severe server-side cache eviction (`antigravity-cli` Issue #1720), per [developer telemetry](https://discuss.ai.google.dev/t/gemini-4-neon-high-cache-misses-on-unchanged-prefix-still-burn-quota-after-nov-21-fix-data-from-nov-22/203824). Across 385 calls (48.7 million uncached input tokens, 39.6 million cache-read tokens), 141 calls above 50K context suffered zero cache reads, generating 45.1 million uncached tokens. Miss rates jump from 10% at 50K–100K tokens (92 calls) and 29% at 100K–250K (100 calls) to 43% at 250K–300K (51 calls) and 65% at 300K–500K (123 calls), multiplying input token costs 20x from the $0.15 cached rate to the $3.00 uncached rate ([Artificial Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/gemini-4-neon-intelligence-gain)).
