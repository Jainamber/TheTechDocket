## 2. India facts (official OpenAI figures + reputable coverage)

- **100 million weekly active ChatGPT users in India** = OpenAI's second-largest market globally (after the US), largest in Asia, ~1 in 8 of ~800M global weekly users. Announced by Sam Altman at the India AI Impact Summit, **February 16, 2026**. Quote: "It has the largest number of students on ChatGPT worldwide, a sign of how many young people here are treating AI as a way to learn faster and get ahead." India also "ranks fourth globally in the use of Prism," OpenAI's free scientific-research tool. → https://www.thehansindia.com/technology/tech-news/india-emerges-as-openais-second-largest-market-with-100-million-weekly-chatgpt-users-1048945
- OpenAI opened its **New Delhi office in August 2025**; Mumbai and Bengaluru offices are slated for 2026. OpenAI appointed **Prabhjeet Singh** (former Uber India & South Asia president) as its **first India managing director**, effective September 2026, reporting to Kiran Mani (APAC MD), overseeing "consumer growth, enterprise adoption, partnerships, regulatory engagement, and operations." Other India personnel: Pragya Misra (ex-Truecaller/Meta) on strategy/global affairs; Rishi Jaitly (ex-Twitter India head) as senior AI-policy adviser. OpenAI also has a data-center partnership with **Tata Group for 100MW of AI data-center capacity**, plus initiatives with Reliance. "18 to 24 year olds account for nearly 50% of ChatGPT usage in India." → https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/26/openai-poaches-uber-india-chief-to-lead-its-biggest-market-outside-the-u-s/
- **ChatGPT Go originated in India.** OpenAI's own blog: "In August 2025, we introduced ChatGPT Go in India as a low-cost subscription... Since then it expanded to 170 additional countries" and is now "our fastest growing plan and among the most affordable AI subscription globally." $8/month is the global reference price; 10x higher limits than free tier; GPT-5.2 Instant model. → https://openai.com/index/introducing-chatgpt-go/
- **ChatGPT Go India price: ₹399/month (~$4.60), launched August 19, 2025**, with UPI payment support. Quote from **Nick Turley, VP at OpenAI**: "Making ChatGPT more affordable has been a key ask from users! We're rolling out Go in India first and will learn from feedback before expanding to other countries." → https://techcrunch.com/2025/08/18/openai-launches-a-sub-5-chatgpt-plan-in-india
- ₹399/month framed elsewhere as "one-fifth the cost of ChatGPT Plus" (₹1,999/month in India). → https://www.timesofai.com/news/openai-rolls-out-chatgpt-go-in-india-for-399/ (Note: this same article's claim of "approximately 1.35 million ChatGPT users" in India looks like a data error/low-quality-source artifact — it contradicts the officially-sourced 100M weekly figure above by two orders of magnitude; not using it.)
- **ChatGPT Go was made free to redeem in India** starting around November 4, 2025 (OpenAI: "please do not cancel your subscription... We may automatically redeem the promotion for you"; offer could "end... at any time without prior notice"). Framed by TechRadar as testing whether US users get the same deal next. → https://www.techradar.com/ai-platforms-assistants/chatgpt/chatgpt-go-is-now-free-in-india-and-u-s-users-are-wondering-if-theyre-next
- **India-focused developer event**: "OpenAI Developer Meetup — New Delhi," June 6, 2026, organized by the OpenAI Developer Community (Parampreet Singh & Shivay Lamba). Free registration; no explicit tie to the official global DevDay 2026 program stated on the page. → https://www.startupgrantsindia.com/events/openai-developer-meetup-new-delhi
- **India-pricing comparison across AI vendors** (published before the July 30 cut, useful competitive-localization context): ChatGPT Go ₹399/mo (~$4.15) with UPI; **Cursor Start ₹649/mo (~$7)** with UPI, quote: "For a long time, our users in India have asked us for two things: pricing that reflects the local market, and the ability to pay with UPI"; **Anthropic Claude** reported at ₹2,000/yr for Pro (~$21) and ₹11,999 for Max (~$125) with **no UPI support** — and the piece notes Anthropic's India prices, once converted, actually exceed US equivalents (straight currency conversion, not true localization) vs. OpenAI/Cursor which "treated India as a market that needed its own plan, at its own price, with its own payment method." → https://thenewstack.io/cursor-anthropic-openai-india-pricing/

## 3. Indian startup / developer API-cost context

- **India IT/AI spending context**: Indian enterprises are "entering a heavy AI investment cycle" — India IT spending projected to grow 6-8% in 2026 vs. 4-6% globally; 40-45% of India's tech-change spend targets AI/data transformation; ~90% of Indian business leaders say their data foundations are inadequate for enterprise-wide AI scaling; 72% of CIOs cite legacy tech debt as the top implementation barrier. Practical cost-routing guidance for Indian founders: redirect simple/classification tasks from Sol ($5/1M input) to Luna (cited pre-cut at $1/1M input in this piece) for an ~80% cost reduction on that line item — "Roughly 95% of enterprise AI usage still runs on frontier models, which means most teams are overpaying by default." This is also the source using ₹95.4/USD (July 10, 2026) for its INR figures. → https://startupfeed.in/cost-efficient-ai-models-price-war-2026/
- **IBM–Sarvam sovereign-AI partnership** (counter-trend data point — India building AI independence rather than just consuming cheaper US tokens), announced **July 31, 2026**: IBM and Sarvam will combine **IBM Sovereign Core** (AI-ready software with governance controls) with **Sarvam's India-first AI stack** (reasoning models, language/voice AI trained in India) for government departments, public-sector bodies, and regulated enterprises, via IBM's GovTech AI Innovation Center in Lucknow. Use cases: citizen services, grievance redressal, document processing, multilingual voice-enabled admin workflows. Quotes: **Sriram Raghavan** (GM, IBM Software India) — "Sovereign AI is not simply about where AI runs. It is about giving organizations control over how AI is governed, deployed, and operated." **Pratyush Kumar** (co-founder, Sarvam) — "IBM's Sovereign Core gives governments an AI-ready technology foundation they control. Our stack puts models, voice, and language technologies on top of it." No pricing/cost figures given in the release. → https://in.newsroom.ibm.com/IBM-and-Sarvam-Collaborate-to-Advance-AI-Sovereignty
- **Sarvam's own competitive response** (same news cycle as the OpenAI cut): Sarvam AI announced plans to build a **one-trillion-parameter foundation model domestically**, launched its "Epoch Builder Edition" platform, introduced "lower AI model pricing than global rivals," and appointed former xAI researcher **Devendra Singh Chaplot** as an advisor on AI research/multilingual capabilities. → https://www.digit.in/news/general/openai-makes-gpt-56-dramatically-cheaper-putting-pressure-on-anthropic-and-rivals.html
- **General (non-India-specific) margin-squeeze context**, useful for the "why token prices keep falling" framing: token prices fell from ~$60 per million tokens (2021) to ~$0.06 today per EpochAI data. Quotes: **Mike Saeks (Cursor CTO)** — "It's like driving a Lamborghini to go to the grocery store to pick up milk when that was designed to be raced around a track." **Marty Kausas (Pylon co-founder)** — described receiving "around $1.6 million in free tokens from one vendor, $65,000 from another and $10,000 from [a] third." **David Casem (Telnyx CEO)** — "It's not like we don't use OpenAI or Anthropic models, we still do. They just don't do everything anymore." Also: Anthropic's inference margins reportedly improved from 38% to over 70% during 2026 on Claude Code adoption; OpenAI's gross margins cited at roughly 33%. → https://www.forbes.com/sites/petercohan/2026/07/28/as-token-costs-plunge-enterprise-ai-providers-face-a-new-margin-squeeze/
- **India price-sensitivity reaction (consumer, not developer)**, unmoderated but real, on the ₹399 ChatGPT Go price specifically: "399 rupees? For that amount I could hire a live in maid and a cook and a personal driver!" with reply "Are you dumb?" — no usernames given by the platform. → https://www.teamblind.com/post/openai-bets-on-india-with-cheapest-chatgpt-plan-at-46-before-global-rollout-q2okq8qu

## 6. Consumer-impact facts (does the cut touch ChatGPT plans/usage limits?)

- **Softonic's own framing (the article specified in the brief)**: covers the cut as an API/enterprise-tier story (GPT-5.6 Luna/Terra models, ChatGPT Work, Codex) — it does **not** state any change to Free/Plus/Pro/Go consumer subscription pricing. Its only consumer-adjacent line: "For those of us who use ChatGPT Work for projects and tasks, it comes down to how usage is counted. Terra and Luna now use fewer credits per task, so we can use them for longer before reaching the limits of our subscription." That statement is scoped to **ChatGPT Work** specifically, not Free/Plus/Pro/Go. → https://en.softonic.com/articles/openai-cuts-gpt-5-6-prices-by-up-to-80-what-does-it-mean-for-usage-limits
- Corroborating: the price cuts "apply automatically across Codex and ChatGPT Work without requiring account migration." → https://www.explainx.ai/blog/openai-gpt-5-6-luna-terra-price-cuts-july-2026
- Background tier/rate-limit data (dated July 2026, **not** confirmed to have changed because of the July 30 cut specifically — cited for baseline context only): on ChatGPT Pro ($200/mo), Luna ~400 msgs/3hrs, Terra ~200 msgs/3hrs, Sol ~80 msgs/3hrs; on Plus ($20/mo), Terra was newly available at 30 msgs/3hrs with Luna/Sol unavailable. → https://tokenkarma.app/blog/openai-rate-limits-july-2026/
- **Net finding: no source found this session states that Free/Plus/Pro/Go sticker prices or headline message caps changed as a direct result of the July 30 Luna/Terra cut.** The cut reads, across every source fetched, as an API/unit-cost change whose consumer-facing effect (if any) is indirect — more usage per credit inside ChatGPT Work, not a change to what individual consumers pay or their raw message limits.
