## 2. THREE BIGGEST CONTENT GAPS

1. **No single source cleanly unifies "delayed vs. still-live" with the *why* (Digital Omnibus) in one glance.** Coverage splits into pure delay pieces (Gibson Dunn, Morgan Lewis) or pure "what's live" pieces (EC official, Jones Walker) — nobody combines both into one table plus explains the confusion caused real harm: TechTimes cites ~78% of obligated organizations took no meaningful compliance action as of June 2026, largely because they conflated the high-risk delay with the (unchanged) Art. 50 transparency deadline. Even axis-intelligence.com's own framing exists to correct an earlier wrong "full enforcement activates" narrative from December 2025.
2. **The India/non-EU-exporter angle is either absent, thin, or commercially captured.** Euronews' global-reach piece — the most "authoritative" global-angle article found — names North America, Asia (28%, no country breakdown), and non-EU Europe, but **India gets zero mention** despite being a top AI/IT-services hub. The only India-specific content is a grant-nonprofit blog (thin on enforcement mechanics/cost) or a vendor selling €2,400/yr Authorised-Representative services (commercially biased, overstates urgency). Nobody has written a neutral "what 2 Aug 2026 enforcement actually means for an Indian SaaS/IT-services exporter" grounded in the actual enforcement start (vs. the stale 2024 AI Pact story that's the only other India-adjacent hit).
3. **The provider-vs-deployer trap and worked fines math are both under-covered together.** TechTimes alone flags that companies building on GPT/Claude/Gemini APIs wrongly assume the model vendor's compliance covers them — a distinct, non-delegable Art. 50 deployer exposure of up to €15M/3% global turnover — but this piece is late (published the day before enforcement) and narrow, not widely ranking. Meanwhile fines coverage (Jaggaer, Legiscope, regdossier) lists the €35M/7%, €15M/3%, €7.5M/1% tiers but only one source shows *any* worked example (a US-framed one), and none combine "you're a deployer, not just relying on your vendor" with a concrete worked fine example for a mid-size non-EU company.
