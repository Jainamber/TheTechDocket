## 3. Export-control claim — VERIFY (event) / KILL (techtimes' specific framing)

**The techtimes.com piece:** [Anthropic Targets $2 Trillion IPO Record; Export Control That Froze June Revenue Remains Active](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/324347/20260813/anthropic-targets-2-trillion-ipo-record-export-control-that-froze-june-revenue-remains-active.htm), Aug 13, 2026.

- **The underlying event is real and heavily corroborated — do not dismiss the whole claim.** **[CONFIRMED]**: a US Commerce Dept./BIS export-control order forced Anthropic to disable **Claude Fable 5 and Mythos 5 worldwide from June 12–30, 2026** (18–19 days) after a jailbreak of the models' guardrails raised cyber-misuse concerns (Amazon reportedly surfaced it). Reported independently by CNBC ([Jun 30](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/30/anthropic-says-trump-admin-has-lifted-export-controls-on-claude-fable-5-and-mythos-5.html)), CNN ([Jun 30](https://www.cnn.com/2026/06/30/tech/anthropic-export-control-ban-lifted-white-house)), Forbes ([Jun 16](https://www.forbes.com/sites/anishasircar/2026/06/16/anthropic-disabled-fable-5-and-mythos-5-after-a-us-export-control-order-heres-what-happened/)), Fortune, Euronews, France24, Al Jazeera, Fox Business, Nextgov, The Record.
- **Revenue-impact claim traces to FT, via ~6 investors: "growth slowed measurably in June"** directly attributed to the ban, with an "extraordinary" rebound once lifted July 1. **[SINGLE-SOURCE]** (FT-origin, repeated by Fortune/Yahoo/Silicon Republic/others without independent re-reporting). Note this is **"slowed," not "froze"** — and Q2 revenue still came in at $11.5B+ overall (more than double Q1's $4.73B), so the sector-standard read is a dip in *growth rate* during the ban window, not a halt to revenue.
- **The "remains active" half of the techtimes headline (as of Aug 13) is CONTRADICTED by reputable reporting.** CNBC, CNN, Fortune, Euronews, France24, Al Jazeera, Fox Business, Nextgov, and The Record all independently confirm the export control was **lifted on June 30, 2026** after Anthropic agreed to added safeguards with Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick — and no reputable outlet reports it being reimposed between then and Aug 17. One non-reputable aggregator (futuresearch.ai) noted the *underlying BIS legal authority* used for the June order hasn't been "structurally resolved" — but that is "the government retains the power to act again," not "the control is currently active," and techtimes' headline conflates the two.
- **Verdict: PARTIAL KILL.** Usable, sourced fact: *"a US export-control order disabled Anthropic's two most advanced models for 18 days in June 2026, and investors told the Financial Times this measurably slowed growth during that window before an extraordinary rebound."* **Do NOT use** techtimes' framing that revenue was "frozen" (overstated vs. "slowed") or that the control "remains active" as of mid-August (flatly contradicted by CNBC/CNN and every other outlet that covered the lift).

## Handle with care in the article

- Every dollar figure above Section 1's confirmed S-1/banks facts (the $2T valuation, October timing, $190–200B 2028 projection, "growth slowed in June") ultimately traces back to **two sourcing chains: FT-via-investors and Reuters/Bloomberg-via-documents** — none of it is Anthropic confirming a number itself. Keep attribution explicit ("investors told the FT," "documents shown to prospective investors," etc.) rather than stating these as settled facts.
- The only Anthropic-on-the-record items are: the S-1 confirmation on its own site (Jun 1) and the "has not decided when or even if" spokesperson line to Reuters. Don't imply Anthropic has confirmed the $2T figure, October timing, or the 2028 projection — it hasn't, per every source found.
- techtimes.com's Aug 13 export-control headline should not be cited/linked as-is (see KILLED #1) — if the export-control episode is mentioned at all, source it to CNBC/CNN/Forbes/FT-derived reporting instead.
