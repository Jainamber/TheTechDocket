## 2. Status of Leading Private Ventures (mid-2026)

**Commonwealth Fusion Systems (CFS)**
- SPARC: first plasma expected later in 2026; CFS targets net energy gain (Q>1) by late 2026–2027 (**company projection**). First superconducting magnet installed Jan 2026; all 18 magnets targeted installed by end of summer 2026. Source: [TechCrunch — magnet installed, Nvidia deal](https://techcrunch.com/2026/01/06/commonwealth-fusion-systems-installs-reactor-magnet-lands-deal-with-nvidia/)
- Google signed a **200MW power purchase agreement** for CFS's planned ARC plant (400MW total capacity), targeting first power to the Virginia grid in the early 2030s. Source: [Data Center Dynamics — Google signs 200MW fusion PPA](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/google-signs-200mw-fusion-ppa-with-commonwealth-fusion-systems/) | [Carbon Credits](https://carboncredits.com/google-backs-fusion-energy-signs-200mw-offtake-agreement-with-commonwealth-fusion-systems/)
- Google DeepMind partnership announced to accelerate plasma simulation/control (Oct 2025). Source: [ANS Nuclear Newswire](https://www.ans.org/news/2025-10-22/article-7484/commonwealth-fusion-systems-partners-with-google-deepmind/)

**Helion Energy**
- 2023 PPA with Microsoft commits Helion to supply **at least 50MW by 2028** (Constellation Energy is power marketer). Broke ground on the Orion plant July 2025; received a Conditional Use Permit Oct 2025, keeping the project "on track" (**company claim**). Source: [S&P Global](https://www.spglobal.com/energy/en/news-research/latest-news/electric-power/073025-helion-energy-breaks-ground-on-fusion-power-plant-slated-to-be-online-in-2028) | [CNBC — original 2023 deal](https://www.cnbc.com/2023/05/10/microsoft-agrees-to-buy-power-from-sam-altman-backed-helion-in-2028.html) | [DCD](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/helion-begins-work-at-fusion-plant-expects-to-deliver-power-to-microsoft-by-2028/)
- Polaris test reactor finished assembly end of 2024, now ramping operations, forming the largest Field Reversed Configuration plasmas Helion has produced, aiming to demonstrate net electricity production (**company claim on timeline**). Source: same DCD link above.
- **Skeptic quote (usable):** Mike Campbell, professor at UC San Diego, said he would be **"happily surprised"** if Helion hits commercial operation by 2028. Source: [E&E News/POLITICO — "Yes, fusion."](https://www.eenews.net/articles/startup-begins-work-on-major-us-fusion-power-plant-yes-fusion/)

**Zap Energy (sheared-flow Z-pinch — closest technical cousin to Fuse's dense plasma focus)**
- FuZE-3 (5th-generation SFS Z-pinch device) exceeded **gigapascal plasma pressures** (Nov 2025). Record pinch currents >600kA, electron temps >2keV, ion temps >2.5keV, **neutron yields exceeding 2×10⁸ per pulse** — described by the company as a 10⁵ (100,000x) improvement in fusion yield since 2021 (**company claim/framing**). Source: [ScienceDaily](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2025/11/251120002836.htm) | [Zap Energy press release](https://www.zapenergy.com/news/zap-energy-exceeds-gigapascal-fusion-plasma-pressures-on-new-fusion-device-fuze-3)
- **Useful comparison for the article:** Zap's ~2×10⁸ neutrons/pulse is roughly 4 orders of magnitude below Fuse Energy's newly claimed 1.27×10¹² neutrons/shot (see Section 4) — both are pulsed-power, magnetically-confined-plasma approaches (Z-pinch vs. dense plasma focus), making this a fair apples-to-apples contrast point.

## 3. Government/Megaproject Status Mid-2026

- **ITER 2024 rebaseline:** New schedule submitted to the ITER Council in June 2024, now the working reference. It **abandons the old "first plasma by 2025" target** entirely; the rebaseline instead frames **research activities starting in 2034**, alongside a reported decade-long delay and a ~€5 billion cost increase. Source: [Max Planck Institute for Plasma Physics — New ITER schedule](https://www.ipp.mpg.de/5434926/ITER_baseline_2024) | [Physics World — decade-long delay, €5bn price hike](https://physicsworld.com/a/iter-fusion-reactor-hit-by-massive-decade-long-delay-and-e5bn-price-hike/)
  - CAUTION: the search snippets use "research activities start 2034" rather than a clean restated "first plasma" date — the new baseline restructured the operational milestones, so don't assert a precise "first plasma = [year]" figure without reading the full Physics World/World Nuclear News piece first.
- **US DOE Milestone-Based Fusion Development Program:** authorized under the Energy Act of 2020, first funded FY2022; 8 awardee companies have collectively raised **>$350M in new private funding** since their May 2023 selection, versus **$46M in federal funding** committed. DOE opened the program to new companies/teams in 2025. Source: [DOE.gov](https://www.energy.gov/articles/us-department-energy-announces-selectees-107-million-fusion-innovation-research-engine) | [ANS/Nuclear Newswire](https://www.ans.org/news/2025-06-12/article-7107/doe-opens-milestone-fusion-pilot-plant-program-to-new-companies-and-teams/)
- **UK STEP programme:** £2.5 billion programme; UK Fusion Energy Ltd launched a new strategy at Fusion Fest 2026 in London; signed a £30M agreement with Dassault Systèmes to build STEP's digital/PLM baseline. Source: [STEP Fusion — new UK strategy](https://stepfusion.com/ukfe-strategy-2026/)

## 4. Fuse Energy's Commercial Positioning

- **Business model:** pulsed-power Dense Plasma Focus (DPF) machines generating near-term revenue from radiation-effects testing for defense and space customers, while working toward commercial fusion power longer-term — explicitly a "sell neutrons now, power later" model, distinct from CFS/Helion/TAE's pure future-power-offtake plays (those companies' 2026 revenue signals are PPAs for power not yet generated — Google/CFS 200MW, Microsoft/Helion 50MW — not product revenue today).
- **Revenue (COMPANY CLAIM):** expects ~$10M from testing devices over the next two years ($2M already contracted); projects **>$300M revenue over the next decade from FAETON II alone** at ~90% gross margins per shot. Source: [Power Magazine — Fuse Touts 'Highest' Neutron Yield](https://www.powermag.com/fuse-touts-highest-neutron-yield-by-any-fusion-company/)
- **Defense contracts:** ongoing contracts with the U.S. Air Force, supported by the Defense Threat Reduction Agency (DTRA) and the Air Force Nuclear Weapons Center, plus other undisclosed DoD customers. Plans to sell shots on TITAN II to DoD and commercial space/electronics customers testing hardware against high-energy X-rays and neutrons. Source: [ExchangeMonitor — Fuse CEO on NNSA/Nevada partnership](https://www.exchangemonitor.com/fuse-ceo-sees-commercial-fusion-accelerating-nnsa-modernization-through-nevada-partnership/) | [Power Magazine link above]
- **The Aug 2026 record itself (COMPANY CLAIM, article's subject):** Fuse's FAETON-X produced **1.27×10¹² neutrons in a single deuterium-deuterium shot** (announced/reported Aug 10, 2026) — Fuse says this is the first publicly documented neutron yield at that level by any commercial fusion company (previously only achieved by national labs), and claims ~**40% more neutrons per megajoule than Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory's MJOLNIR facility** — this LLNL comparison is Fuse's own framing, not independently verified in search results. Source: [Power Magazine](https://www.powermag.com/fuse-touts-highest-neutron-yield-by-any-fusion-company/) | [The Fusion Report — This Week's Fusion News: August 14, 2026](https://thefusionreport.substack.com/p/this-weeks-fusion-news-august-14) | company site [f.energy/library](https://www.f.energy/library)

## 5. India Fusion Involvement

- India is a full ITER member via the Department of Atomic Energy (DAE); **ITER-India** operates under the **Institute for Plasma Research (IPR), Gandhinagar**, and is responsible for delivering India's in-kind contributions (in-wall shielding blocks, diagnostic neutral beam systems, high-voltage power supplies). India contributes **~9.1% of ITER's construction cost**, same as each non-host member. Source: [ITER-India — India's contribution to ITER](https://www.iterindia.in/indias-contribution-iter) | [ITER-India overview](https://www.iterindia.in/overview)
- 2026 event: the **16th ITER International School (IIS 2026)** will be held **Nov 30–Dec 4, 2026 in Bengaluru**, jointly organized by IPR Gandhinagar and ICTS-TIFR Bengaluru with the ITER Organization and Aix-Marseille University; theme "Tokamaks as Fusion Reactors." Source: [iis2026.ipr.res.in](https://iis2026.ipr.res.in/) | [ITER.org announcement](https://www.iter.org/node/20687/16th-iter-international-school-announced-india-tokamaks-fusion-reactors)
- **No evidence found** of any India-based private commercial fusion startup, India-linked investment in Fuse Energy or other private ventures, or any 2026 India news connected to the commercial dense-plasma-focus/private-fusion race this article covers. India's fusion presence remains entirely on the government/ITER megaproject side.
- **Recommendation:** only mention India if the article wants a brief factual aside (e.g., contrasting India's ITER-megaproject role with the commercial DPF race Fuse/Zap are running) — do not stretch this into a "party role" in the Fuse Energy story itself. Fine to skip entirely per the original brief.

## 6. Timeline Reality-Check Quotes

- FIA survey data (2026 report, 53 private companies polled): consensus is fusion should start delivering grid power in the **2030–2035** window; over half of companies anticipate delivering power to the grid in the early 2030s, three-quarters by the end of that decade. **83% of companies surveyed (2025) say securing investment remains a major challenge**, and the industry estimates it needs **another $77 billion** to build first commercial plants. Source: [Enlit World — Fusion energy expected in early 2030s indicates survey](https://www.enlit.world/library/fusion-energy-expected-in-early-2030s-indicates-survey)
- **Quotable skeptic line:** Mike Campbell (UC San Diego professor) on Helion's 2028 Microsoft target — would be **"happily surprised"** if they hit it. Source: [E&E News/POLITICO](https://www.eenews.net/articles/startup-begins-work-on-major-us-fusion-power-plant-yes-fusion/)
- **Broader-horizon framing:** "a reasonable expectation is that fusion contributes a meaningful share of electricity generation sometime between **2040 and 2050**" — useful counterweight to company-stated 2028/2030 targets. Source: [Earth911 — The State of Fusion Energy in 2026: Real Reactors, Real Grids, Real Caveats](https://earth911.com/eco-tech/the-state-of-fusion-energy-in-2026-real-reactors-real-grids-real-caveats/)

## Things to avoid / handle carefully in the article

1. **Don't state a precise ITER "first plasma" year** without reading the full source — the 2024 rebaseline restructured the milestones and the clean "2034" figure found refers to "research activities," not a crisply restated first-plasma date.
2. **Don't conflate Proxima Fusion's two funding figures** (€130M Series A vs. $518M 2026 raise) without checking dates — likely different rounds.
3. **All Fuse Energy performance/revenue numbers are the company's own claims** (including the LLNL MJOLNIR per-megajoule comparison) — flag as such in copy, per outlet coverage (Power Magazine, The Fusion Report) rather than independent verification.
4. **Zap Energy's "10⁵ improvement since 2021" is Zap's own framing** from its press release, not third-party verified.
5. **India has no genuine role in the Fuse Energy story or the private commercial fusion race** — its only verifiable fusion presence is the government ITER megaproject via IPR Gandhinagar. Do not manufacture an India angle beyond that.
