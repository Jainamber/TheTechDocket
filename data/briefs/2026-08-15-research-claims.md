# Fact-Verification Brief: Fuse Energy Technologies "FAETON-X" 1.27e12 Neutron Yield Claim

Verification run: 2026-08-15 IST. Method: WebSearch only (WebFetch blocked). Facts not found in search snippets are marked UNVERIFIED per instructions — no estimation.

## 1. Core claim: 1.27e12 neutrons, D-D, single-shot, announcement date

- CLAIM: FAETON-X produced peak yield of 1.27 trillion (1.27×10¹²) D-D neutrons in a single shot → **VERIFIED** — preprint states "(1.27±0.27)×10¹² neutrons/shot at 10 Torr deuterium," identified as a **peak recorded** value, not a routine/average result — https://www.researchsquare.com/article/rs-10657590/v1
- CLAIM: This is a peak, not typical, performance — device "not yet fully conditioned" typically produces ~5×10¹¹ neutrons/shot → **VERIFIED** (important nuance often dropped in coverage: the 1.27e12 figure is the single best shot, not the operating norm) — https://www.researchsquare.com/article/rs-10657590/v1
- CLAIM: Announced August 11, 2026 → **VERIFIED** — "Fuse Energy Technologies Corp. on August 11 published a report noting the company set a record..." — https://www.powermag.com/fuse-touts-highest-neutron-yield-by-any-fusion-company/
- CLAIM: Framed as "first fusion company, private or commercial, to publicly document a neutron yield in the 10¹² range" → **VERIFIED** (this is the company's own framing, reported by PowerMag) — https://www.powermag.com/fuse-touts-highest-neutron-yield-by-any-fusion-company/
- Note on phrasing: "measured" vs "claimed" — the 1.27e12 figure comes from Fuse's own preprint (self-reported, not yet peer-reviewed — see §4), not an independent third-party measurement. All coverage traces back to this same preprint/company report.

## 2. MJOLNIR comparison

- CLAIM: LLNL has a DPF device named MJOLNIR → **VERIFIED** — "MJOLNIR (MegaJOuLe Neutron Imaging Radiography) is a Dense Plasma Focus (DPF) being developed by LLNL as a prototype to assess the viability of a DPF as a flash neutron radiography source" — https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2019APS..DPPJ10093P/abstract and https://www.llnl.gov/article/50001/radiography-innovation-shows-new-promise-experimental-dense-plasma-focus-milestone
- CLAIM: MJOLNIR yield/energy figures → **VERIFIED** — "MJOLNIR has discharged up to 1.3 MJ of stored energy into a deuterium plasma load and achieved yields up to 1.2×10¹² neutrons per discharge" — https://www.osti.gov/biblio/1988207 ; LLNL's own site separately notes the MJOLNIR team "achieved greater than 10^12 neutrons in a single deuterium gas shot" — https://www.llnl.gov/article/50001/radiography-innovation-shows-new-promise-experimental-dense-plasma-focus-milestone
- CLAIM: "~40% more neutrons per MJ than MJOLNIR" → **PARTIAL / plausible but company-framed**. Arithmetic check using the independently-sourced LLNL numbers above: MJOLNIR ≈ 1.2×10¹²/1.3 MJ ≈ 0.92×10¹² n/MJ; FAETON-X ≈ 1.27×10¹²/1 MJ ≈ 1.27×10¹² n/MJ → ratio ≈ 1.38, i.e. ~38% higher — in the ballpark of the claimed "~40%." However, the comparison itself (which MJOLNIR shot/energy point to use as the baseline) is **Fuse's own framing**, not an independent analyst's calculation — "Fuse officials said the company's latest work 'exceeds the fusion neutron yield per MJ of the world's largest government plasma focus facilities'" — https://www.powermag.com/fuse-touts-highest-neutron-yield-by-any-fusion-company/. No independent (non-Fuse, non-LLNL) third party was found benchmarking the two devices head-to-head.
- Red flag: MJOLNIR is a research/prototype device still being upgraded ("Upgrades To LLNL's MJOLNIR... Power Flow Region and Mounting Structure," 2023) — https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2023APS..DPPPP1082W/abstract — so the comparison may be to a specific historical MJOLNIR configuration/shot rather than MJOLNIR's best-ever or most current performance. Could not verify from snippets which exact MJOLNIR shot Fuse used as its baseline.

- CLAIM: FAETON-X is a dense plasma focus (DPF) device → **VERIFIED** — "single-pulse flash neutron source purpose-built as a pulsed power fusion R&D and radiation effects testing platform with both D-D and D-T fusion fuels" — https://www.researchsquare.com/article/rs-10657590/v1

Do not assume co-location without a direct source.

## 7. Prior DPF neutron-yield records / other private-company claims

- CLAIM: National labs achieved high DPF neutron yields historically → **VERIFIED**, multiple data points:
  - NNSS **Gemini DPF**: "yielded approximately 8×10¹¹ deuterium-deuterium (DD) neutrons per pulse" on a "2 MJ 6 MA facility that became operational in 2009" — https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/1741025 (also referenced in https://www.science.gov/topicpages/d/dense+plasma+focus context)
  - **LLNL MJOLNIR**: up to 1.2×10¹² neutrons per discharge at 1.3 MJ (see §2) — https://www.osti.gov/biblio/1988207 ; a separate note states "recent shots achiev[ed] up to 4×10¹¹ neutrons per pulse at 2.2 MA peak current" in an earlier/different MJOLNIR configuration or optimization stage — https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2019APS..DPPJ10093P/abstract — the multiple different MJOLNIR figures across sources (4×10¹¹ vs 1.2×10¹²) suggest performance improved over successive campaigns/upgrades; exact chronology not fully resolved from snippets.
  - So ranking found so far: FAETON-X (1.27×10¹²) > MJOLNIR (up to 1.2×10¹²) > Gemini (~8×10¹¹) — all in the same order-of-magnitude 10¹¹–10¹² band, all D-D.

## Summary red flags for editorial

1. The central 1.27e12 figure is a **peak single-shot result** from an as-yet **unconditioned, not-peer-reviewed** device — typical/routine yield is described in the same preprint as ~5×10¹¹ (less than half the headline number).
2. The "~40% more than MJOLNIR" comparison, while arithmetically plausible against the specific LLNL figures found (1.2×10¹² @ 1.3 MJ), is a **company-authored comparison** against a specific (possibly not most-recent/best) MJOLNIR data point — no independent third party validated this comparison.
3. **Do not confuse "Fuse Energy" (UK retail energy, $70M Series B/$5B valuation, ex-Revolut founders) with "Fuse Energy Technologies Corporation" (US fusion/DPF company, JC Btaiche, San Leandro CA)** — they are unrelated companies with near-identical names; several aggregators (e.g. Tracxn) blend their data.
4. Funding-raised figures for the actual fusion company are inconsistent across secondary sources ($52M per 2024 Not Boring piece vs $92.7M per Tracxn) and **no primary press release with dated round/amount was found** to adjudicate.
5. No independent outlet, physicist critique, or forum discussion (HN/Reddit) specific to this claim was found in this search pass — coverage so far appears to be essentially one story (Fuse's own preprint) relayed through one trade outlet (PowerMag) and one aggregator newsletter (The Fusion Report), i.e. thin independent scrutiny to date.
6. Exact physical location/site of the FAETON-X device itself (as opposed to general corporate HQ) is unconfirmed.
