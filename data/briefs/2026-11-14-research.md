# Research Pass: Nvidia Circular Financing & "Engineered Scarcity" Allegations

**Date of Research:** November 14, 2026  
**Topic:** Nvidia allegedly engineered GPU scarcity by funding AI startups and neoclouds to buy its own chips, propping up demand while facing a massive supply of TSMC wafers.

---

## 1. Core Narrative & Key Events
*   **The Catalyst:** On November 13, 2026, *The Wall Street Journal* (reporters Anissa Gardizy and Robbie Whelan) published an exposé alleging that Nvidia artificially inflated the perception of GPU scarcity. The report claims Nvidia funneled billions of dollars into AI startups and "neocloud" providers (e.g., CoreWeave, Nebius, Lambda) so they could purchase Nvidia GPUs and build data centers.
*   **The Motivation:** The WSJ report alleges Nvidia was facing an "avalanche" of incoming GPU and memory supply from TSMC with no immediate buyers, as major hyperscalers (Amazon, Google, Meta, Microsoft) shifted toward custom silicon. To maintain high demand optics and move excess silicon, Nvidia engaged in aggressive vendor financing (dubbed "circular financing" or the "Bank of Nvidia").
*   **The August 2026 Clash:** In August 2026, then-CEO Jensen Huang called a meeting with senior finance executives. CFO Colette Kress presented numbers and pushed back on these high-risk financing guarantees. Huang reportedly cut her off, calling her "a deer in the headlights" and dismissing her as a mere "reporting CFO" who could not help shape strategy. 
*   **Current Status:** Jensen Huang resigned on November 12, 2026, following a board probe into extramarital affairs and potential national security risks. Colette Kress has been appointed interim CEO. She now inherits the very web of multi-billion-dollar commitments she privately fought to scale back.

### 1. Global Context & Core Story

*   **The Allegation:** On November 13, 2026, a *Wall Street Journal* report alleged that Nvidia artificially engineered GPU scarcity by funding AI startups and small-scale cloud providers to buy its own GPUs and build data centers. This "circular financing" (or "fundgineering") was designed to prop up demand signals and move an oversupply of semiconductors from TSMC as hyperscalers shifted to custom chips.
*   **Internal Conflict:** Nvidia CFO and acting/interim CEO Colette Kress reportedly pushed back against these circular financing agreements in an August 2026 meeting, warning that some funded startups couldn't afford the GPUs and would become financial dead ends. She also urged scaling back Nvidia's reported $250 billion commitment to OpenAI. Former CEO Jensen Huang (who resigned around November 11–12, 2026, amid a board investigation into an affair with a suspected Chinese spy) allegedly called Kress a "deer in the headlights" during this exchange.
*   **Status:** **REPORTED_SECONDARY** (Attributed to *Wall Street Journal* reporting; Nvidia has not confirmed these internal exchanges or circular financing metrics in official filings).
