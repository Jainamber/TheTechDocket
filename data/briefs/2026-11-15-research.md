## 1. The Core Story: The WSJ Investigation & Executive Shakeup
*   **The Catalyst:** Former NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang officially resigned on November 12, 2026, after 33 years leading the company. Longtime CFO Colette Kress was appointed interim CEO.
*   **The WSJ Report:** An investigative report published by *The Wall Street Journal* on November 12–13, 2026, exposed a deep internal rift over NVIDIA’s aggressive "circular financing" (or vendor financing) strategy.
*   **The August 2026 Clash:** During a tense finance meeting in August 2026, CFO Colette Kress pushed to scale back NVIDIA’s massive credit guarantees and circular financing commitments to smaller cloud providers ("neoclouds") and major AI labs. Huang reportedly cut her off mid-presentation, belittled her as a "reporting CFO" rather than a strategic partner, and called her "a deer in the headlights".
*   **The "Avalanche" Rationale:** Huang privately justified the financing spree by admitting that major hyperscalers (Amazon, Google, Microsoft) were quietly moving away from off-the-shelf NVIDIA chips in favor of custom in-house silicon. Meanwhile, NVIDIA was on the hook for an "avalanche" of prepaid silicon wafers and memory it had committed to buy from TSMC. To prevent this surplus from flooding the open market and dragging GPU prices down, Huang funded AI startups and neoclouds so they would buy NVIDIA hardware.

# Research Notes: NVIDIA Circular Financing & Scarcity Allegations (2026-11-15)

These grounding notes compile verified metrics, primary disclosures, and secondary reporting regarding the allegations that NVIDIA engineered GPU scarcity by funding its own customers.

## 1. The Core Allegations & Corporate Shakeup

*   **The Catalyst:** On November 12, 2026, NVIDIA co-founder and CEO Jensen Huang resigned effective immediately (board accepted on November 11). His departure followed revelations of an extramarital affair with Alice Huang, a former executive at Singapore-based data center operator Megaspeed, whom U.S. authorities identified as a Chinese intelligence asset. CFO Colette Kress was named interim CEO.
*   **The WSJ Investigative Report:** On November 12–13, 2026, *The Wall Street Journal* published an exposé alleging that NVIDIA systematically provided financing and credit guarantees to AI startups and smaller cloud service providers (neoclouds) that could not afford its GPUs.
*   **The "Circular Financing" Loop:** NVIDIA provided capital or credit guarantees to these entities, which then used the funds to buy NVIDIA GPUs. This money looped back to NVIDIA as revenue, artificially sustaining the perception of high demand and keeping GPU prices high.
*   **The "Scarcity" Claim vs. Reality:** 
    *   *TechPowerUp* (Nov 13, 2026) framed this as NVIDIA "engineering scarcity" to inflate consumer GPU and memory prices.
    *   **[UNVERIFIED GAP: No Evidence of Scarcity Manipulation]** There is no evidence in the WSJ report or SEC filings that NVIDIA deliberately restricted GPU or memory supply to lift retail prices. The circular financing was a response to *oversupply* (an impending "avalanche" of chips), not a plot to manufacture a shortage.
*   **The Structural Dilemma:** Hyperscalers (Meta, Google, Microsoft, Amazon) have been accelerating their shift to custom in-house chips (Google TPU, Amazon Trainium, Microsoft Maia), reducing their reliance on off-the-shelf NVIDIA GPUs. Meanwhile, NVIDIA had massive, non-cancelable wafer and memory commitments at TSMC. Huang internally described this incoming supply as an impending "avalanche". To absorb it, he launched a financing spree to create buyers.
*   **The August 2026 Boardroom Clash:** 
    *   **[REPORTED_SECONDARY (WSJ)]** In August 2026, Huang convened his senior finance team. CFO Colette Kress pushed to scale back circular financing agreements and questioned how to value the guarantees. 
    *   Huang reportedly cut her off mid-presentation, calling her a "deer in the headlights" and a "reporting CFO" who was not equipped to help shape strategy.
    *   **Nico Caprez:** Some of these financing deals were led by Huang's son-in-law, Nico Caprez, who became VP of global AI infrastructure growth in January 2026. Kress's team had reportedly tried to rein in his activities.

## 2. The OpenAI Ohio Data Center Deal

*   **The Project:** Sited at the PORTS-Pike Technology Campus in Pike County, Ohio (on a decommissioned federal uranium-enrichment site). Sized at 8 IT-gigawatts (GW) of computing capacity (10 GW total power), developed by SoftBank's SB Energy with OpenAI as the tenant under a 20-year lease.
*   **The Guarantee Dispute:** 
    *   **[REPORTED_SECONDARY (WSJ/Reuters)]** NVIDIA initially considered providing a massive $250 billion financial backstop/guarantee to help OpenAI secure loans (since OpenAI lacks an investment-grade credit rating).
    *   Kress and her finance team raised concerns about the scale of the exposure and successfully scaled it down.
*   **The Final Deal:** 
    *   **[CONFIRMED_PRIMARY (SEC Form 8-K, August 17, 2026)]** NVIDIA entered into multiple residual-value guarantees with SB Energy relating to leases for approximately 4.25 GW of IT load.
    *   NVIDIA's aggregate payment obligation is cumulatively capped at **$105 billion** for the initial commitment.
    *   NVIDIA is also investing **$1.5 billion** directly in SB Energy and will be the exclusive provider of AI computing infrastructure at the campus.
    *   The first phase (800 MW) is scheduled to come online in 2028.
    *   **The Mechanism:** If OpenAI defaults, NVIDIA covers the shortfall between the guaranteed minimum value of the lease and what SB Energy can recover by re-leasing or selling the site.
