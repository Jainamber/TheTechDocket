# Research Pass: Google Gemini Personal Data Routing to Human Reviewers

## 1. Core Findings & Timeline of Reversal
*   **September 19, 2023 (Launch):** Google launched Bard (now Gemini) Workspace extensions with an explicit privacy promise: *"If you choose to use the Workspace extensions, your content from Gmail, Docs and Drive is not seen by human reviewers, used by Bard to show you ads or used to train the Bard model."* **[CONFIRMED_PRIMARY: Google Official Blog]**
*   **October 10, 2025 (Default-On Shift):** Google silently switched Gemini "Smart features" from opt-in to on-by-default across Gmail, Chat, and Meet accounts, enabling AI scanning of private communications without explicit user consent. **[REPORTED_SECONDARY: Court Filings / Lawsuit]**
*   **November 11, 2025 (Class Action Filed):** *Thele v. Google LLC* (Case No. 5:25-cv-09704, N.D. Cal., San Jose, before Judge Noël Wise) was filed, alleging that this default-on change constituted unauthorized wiretapping under the California Invasion of Privacy Act (CIPA). **[CONFIRMED_PRIMARY: Court Docket]**
*   **July 7, 2026 (Initial Dismissal):** Judge Wise dismissed the *Thele* complaint for lack of Article III standing because plaintiffs failed to show "concrete harm" (i.e., they only alleged that Gemini *could* track their data, not what specific data was accessed). **[REPORTED_SECONDARY: Bloomberg Law / Legal Analysis]**
*   **August 18, 2026 (Amended Complaint):** Plaintiffs filed a second amended complaint. **[CONFIRMED_PRIMARY: Court Docket]**
*   **September 29, 2026 (Motion to Dismiss):** Google filed a motion to dismiss the second amended complaint, which remains pending. **[CONFIRMED_PRIMARY: Court Docket]**
*   **October 16, 2026 (Silent Policy Reversal):** Google quietly edited its September 2023 blog post, adding a dated footnote: *"Update, October 16, 2026: ... If users choose to enable these new features, data from Workspace Apps will be processed to personalize other Google experiences, and depending on a user's settings, that same subset of data can be used to train generative AI models. It can also be subject to human review in limited circumstances."* **[CONFIRMED_PRIMARY: Google Official Blog]**
*   **November 8–10, 2026 (Technical Leak Documentation):** A GitHub repository titled `thisisme6598/gemini_files` went viral on Hacker News (720+ points, 140+ comments), documenting that consumer Gemini with Connected Apps enabled autonomously ingests personal files and emails into human review pipelines. **[REPORTED_SECONDARY: GitHub / Hacker News]**

## 2. The Reversal Details (Verbatim Quotes & Sources)
*   **Google Help Answer 16836988 ("About personalization with Connected Apps"):**
    *   *On Human Review:* *"When connected to Gemini, a subset of Connected Apps data processed by Gemini is reviewed by human reviewers (including trained service providers) to debug our services, help keep them safe, and improve them."* **[CONFIRMED_PRIMARY: Google Help Center]**
    *   *On What Reviewers See:* *"Interactions, summaries, excerpts and inferences from your relevant emails and files. In some cases, for example, where the email or file is brief or particularly relevant to respond to the prompt, the summary may just be the email or file."* **[CONFIRMED_PRIMARY: Google Help Center]**
    *   *The Warning:* *"Please don't connect your apps to Gemini Apps if they contain confidential information that you wouldn't want a reviewer to see."* **[CONFIRMED_PRIMARY: Google Help Center]**
*   **Data Retention:** Conversations selected for human review are disconnected from the user's Google Account but are retained for **up to three years**, even if the user deletes their Gemini Apps activity. **[CONFIRMED_PRIMARY: Gemini Apps Privacy Hub]**
*   **Account Scope:** This policy applies strictly to **Consumer Google Accounts** (personal `@gmail.com` accounts). Google Workspace commercial accounts (Business, Enterprise, and Education) operate under separate Cloud Data Processing Addenda (DPA) that contractually exclude human review and external generative training by default. **[CONFIRMED_PRIMARY: Google Workspace Terms]**

## 3. Technical Behavior (GitHub Leak Documentation)
*   **Ambient Triggering:** Casual mentions of personal topics (e.g., trips, bills, names, reservations) in conversation autonomously trigger background Workspace lookups without requiring explicit `@Drive` or `@Gmail` tags. **[REPORTED_SECONDARY: GitHub `thisisme6598/gemini_files`]**
*   **Probabilistic Ingestion Pipeline:** Because LLM tool-calling is nondeterministic, identical queries can yield different results across sessions, falling into four distinct scenarios:
    1.  *Zero Retrieval:* No tool is triggered; no personal data is pulled.
    2.  *Metadata / Generic Summary:* Gemini pulls files/emails and generates a high-level summary (e.g., *"You have 3 emails regarding your flight"*).
    3.  *Partial Unredacted Extraction:* Gemini extracts a 2–4 sentence exact quotation, often containing sensitive PII, account details, or specific figures.
    4.  *Full-File Ingestion:* The complete, unredacted text of the document or email is ingested directly into the active session context, making it eligible for human review.
*   **Scrubber Failures:** Google's automatic regex and Named Entity Recognition (NER) scrubbers fail to sufficiently de-identify narrative data. Account numbers, confirmation codes, transaction dates, and combinations of personal identifiers (e.g., name-date-event combos) survive filtering intact and are sent to human reviewers. **[REPORTED_SECONDARY: GitHub `thisisme6598/gemini_files`]**

## 4. The India Angle & Quantitative Math
*   **Pricing & Tiers in India:**
    *   **Google AI Pro (Gemini Advanced):** Priced at **₹1,950 per month**. It bundles access to Google's flagship reasoning models (Gemini 4 Argon) with 2TB (recently upgraded to 5TB for some tiers) of Google One cloud storage and deep Workspace integration.
    *   **Google AI Plus:** A budget-friendly tier priced at **₹399 per month** (introductory price of ₹199/month for the first six months) that includes 200GB (or 400GB via App Store) of storage and mid-tier model access.
*   **Taxation & Billing Math:**
    *   Google publishes India prices in rupees, and Google Play India prices are **inclusive of GST**.
    *   The transaction is billed through **Google Asia Pacific Pte. Ltd.** (based in Singapore, GSTIN: `9917SGP29001OST`).
    *   Because prices are GST-inclusive and billed directly in INR, Indian users paying via local methods (like UPI) pay exactly **₹1,950/month** with **0% bank forex markup** and no separate tax lines at checkout.
*   **The Jio Arbitrage:**
    *   Millions of Reliance Jio users on qualifying 5G plans (₹349/month or above) receive up to **18 months of Google AI Pro (Gemini Advanced) entirely free**. This massive zero-cost user base is highly vulnerable to silent data routing if they enable Connected Apps.
*   **DPDP Act Compliance (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023):**
    *   **Section 6 Consent Violation:** Under the DPDP Act, consent must be *free, specific, informed, unconditional, and unambiguous* with a clear affirmative action. Google's default-on activation of Gemini "Smart features" in October 2025 violates this, as pre-ticked boxes and silent defaults do not constitute valid consent.
    *   **Enterprise Risk:** Under the DPDP Act, Indian enterprises are legally responsible for any personal data their employees share with third-party AI tools. If employees access consumer Gemini accounts (governed by standard terms) on corporate devices, sensitive corporate and customer data can be routed to human reviewers, exposing the company to severe penalties of up to **₹250 crore** per breach.

## 5. How to Opt Out
*   **Step 1: Disconnect Google Workspace**
    *   *On Desktop:* Go to `gemini.google.com` -> Settings -> Personal Intelligence -> Connected Apps -> Disconnect Google Workspace.
    *   *On Mobile:* Open Gemini app -> Tap Profile Picture -> Personal Intelligence -> Connected Apps -> Toggle off Google Workspace.
*   **Step 2: Disable "Keep Activity"**
    *   Go to Gemini Apps Activity settings and turn off "Keep Activity". Note: This stops future chats from being used for training/review, but does not delete previously reviewed data (which is retained for 3 years).

## 6. Anti-Leakage Verification & Gaps
*   **[CONFIRMED_PRIMARY]** Google's October 16, 2026 blog post update and Help Answer 16836988 explicitly confirm that connected Gmail/Drive data is routed to human reviewers and can be used for model training.
*   **[CONFIRMED_PRIMARY]** The *Thele v. Google* class action docket and Google's pending motion to dismiss are verified public records.
*   **[UNVERIFIED GAP: Technical Testing]** The specific technical behavior documented in the `thisisme6598/gemini_files` repository (ambient triggering, the 4-tier ingestion pipeline, and scrubber failures) has been widely reported and discussed on Hacker News, but has not been independently verified by Google or a formal third-party security audit. It should be framed as "documented by researchers" rather than an absolute, universally proven system behavior.
