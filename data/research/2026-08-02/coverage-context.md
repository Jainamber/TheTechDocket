## Caveats/criticisms

- **Benchmark self-reporting / unverifiable harness**: Tech Times reports that "DeepSeek's benchmark harness remains unreleased, preventing independent verification," and that prior testing had shown V4-Pro's self-reported scores "significantly outpaced independently measured performance" (paraphrase of the fetched summary). This is a credibility caveat specifically about the July 31 benchmark claims. Source: https://www.techtimes.com/articles/322513/20260731/deepseek-retrained-v4-flash-beats-its-flagship-pro-nine-agent-benchmarks.htm
- **High hallucination rate reported (April 2026 preview build)**: Artificial Analysis reported "V4 Pro and V4 Flash both have a very high hallucination rate of 94% and 96% respectively" on their index (quoted as returned by the fetch). This figure is from the April 2026 preview article and its methodology (i.e., which specific Artificial Analysis test this refers to) was not detailed in the fetched summary — flagged as needing methodology context before quoting in the piece. Source: https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/deepseek-is-back-among-the-leading-open-weights-models-with-v4-pro-and-v4-flash
- **Benchmark cross-comparison skepticism**: raised independently by Hacker News commenters in both threads — questioning whether harnesses are equivalent across models when comparing scores. Source: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49119559
- **Censorship of politically sensitive topics**: Wikipedia's "DeepSeek (chatbot)" article states the platform "restricts responses on politically sensitive topics, including the Tiananmen Square massacre and Taiwan's political status" (paraphrase). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DeepSeek_(chatbot)
- **HN pushback on the censorship framing**: a commenter in the first HN thread argued "Western models censor just as much shit as the Chinese models do" (quoted as returned by the fetch) — included here as a real, attributed community counterpoint, not as a verified factual claim. Source: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49120299
- **Data transfer / privacy**: per Wikipedia, South Korea's data regulator "confirmed DeepSeek sent the nation's user data to ByteDance in China," announcing in April 2025 that personal information on "over a million South Koreans" had been transferred (paraphrase). This predates V4 Flash by over a year and refers to the DeepSeek chatbot/app generally, not the V4 Flash model specifically — included as ongoing background context outlets/critics may invoke. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DeepSeek_(chatbot)
- **Historical data breach**: Wikipedia states researchers found an exposed DeepSeek database on Jan 29, 2025 containing "more than a million sensitive entries," including chat logs, API keys, and backend operational details (paraphrase). Same caveat as above about dating. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DeepSeek_(chatbot)
- **Government bans**: per Wikipedia, Australia, South Korea, Canada, Italy, and the Czech Republic have restricted DeepSeek on government devices citing security concerns (paraphrase, dates not specified in the fetched summary). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DeepSeek_(chatbot)
- **Enterprise risk framing**: InfoWorld's piece (analyst Amit Jaju of Ankura Consulting) flags that enterprises need to weigh "data sovereignty, IP leakage, and regulatory compliance risks when using Chinese-origin AI systems" (paraphrase). Source: https://www.infoworld.com/article/4176709/deepseeks-steep-v4-pro-price-cut-escalates-ai-pricing-war.html
- **Feature gap noted by community**: lack of multimodal input support in V4 Flash compared to GPT-5.6, per an HN commenter. Source: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49120299
