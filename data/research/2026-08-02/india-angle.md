# DeepSeek V4 Flash — The India Angle
Research compiled 2026-08-02. DeepSeek released the official "V4-Flash-0731" build on **July 31, 2026** (confirmed by Caixin Global, Aug 1 2026: https://www.caixinglobal.com/2026-08-01/deepseek-releases-official-v4-flash-model-as-chinas-ai-race-intensifies-102470292.html and MarkTechPost, July 31 2026: https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/07/31/deepseek-upgrades-deepseek-v4-flash-0731-with-major-agentic-and-coding-gains/). No source found ties this specific July 31, 2026 release to an India-specific announcement — the India material below concerns DeepSeek's broader presence in India (app, R1/V3 self-hosting, policy fights) into which V4 Flash now lands.

## Verified facts

- **DeepSeek V4-Flash-0731 pricing (USD), released July 31, 2026:** $0.14 per 1M input tokens (cache miss), $0.0028 per 1M input tokens (cache hit), $0.28 per 1M output tokens — price held flat from the prior preview build. Sources: official DeepSeek pricing page (fetched, pricing dated July 25, 2026) https://deepseek.ai/pricing ; MarkTechPost (July 31, 2026) https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/07/31/deepseek-upgrades-deepseek-v4-flash-0731-with-major-agentic-and-coding-gains/ ; OfficeChai — Indian tech outlet (n.d., July/Aug 2026) https://officechai.com/ai/deepseek-releases-deepseek-v4-flash-0731-gives-opus-4-8-level-performance-at-a-fraction-of-the-price/ ; XenoSpectrum "price held at $0.14" https://xenospectrum.com/en/deepseek-v4-flash-0731-pricing/

- **USD/INR reference rate: ~95.4** as of July 31–Aug 1, 2026. Sources: Trading Economics (rate 95.4035–95.4890, dated July 31, 2026) https://tradingeconomics.com/india/currency ; X-Rates.com monthly average table (Aug 2026 = 95.42, 1-day figure) https://www.x-rates.com/average/?from=USD&to=INR&amount=1&year=2026
  - *Derived conversion (my own arithmetic on the two cited figures above, not itself sourced to a single publisher):* at ~₹95.4/$1, V4-Flash-0731 works out to roughly **₹13.4 per 1M input tokens (cache miss), ₹0.27 per 1M (cache hit), and ₹26.7 per 1M output tokens.**

- **India was the single largest source of DeepSeek app downloads globally** in the weeks after its January 2025 launch, accounting for 15.6% of all downloads across platforms since launch, as the app hit 16 million global downloads in 18 days (nearly double ChatGPT's initial 9 million). Source: Business Standard, Feb 1, 2025: https://www.business-standard.com/world-news/deepseek-ai-india-global-app-store-downloads-security-concerns-125020100373_1.html — **NOTE: this is Jan/Feb 2025 data, tied to the R1 launch, not a 2026 or V4-Flash-specific figure.** Sensor Tower's "State of Mobile 2026" report (via 9to5Mac, Jan 21, 2026) ranks DeepSeek third among globally downloaded AI apps for 2025 (after ChatGPT and Gemini) but gives no India-specific breakout: https://9to5mac.com/2026/01/21/sensor-towers-state-of-mobile-2026-tiktok-dominates-ai-apps-surge-games-lose-ground/

- **India's Finance Ministry told employees not to use ChatGPT and DeepSeek on official devices**, citing data-security concerns, in a circular reported around Feb 5–6, 2025. Sources: WION (Feb 2025) https://www.wionews.com/india-news/indian-finance-ministry-bans-employees-from-using-chatgpt-deepseek-report-8692452 ; Onmanorama (Feb 6, 2025) https://www.onmanorama.com/news/india/2025/02/06/finance-ministry-chatgpt-deepseek-ai-ban-security-data-india.html ; Trak.in https://trak.in/stories/finance-ministry-bans-chatgpt-deepseek-other-ai-tools/ — **this is a device-use directive for government employees, not a nationwide public ban on the app.** I could not verify whether this circular is still formally in force as of Aug 2026 (see Unverified).

- **The Delhi High Court is actively scrutinizing DeepSeek over data-privacy/national-security concerns via a pending PIL.** As of the most recent reporting found (Oct 30, 2025), the court directed the Union government to file a detailed affidavit within four weeks; MeitY told the court it is developing AI governance guidelines under the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023, and is considering "a licensing and compliance framework for foreign AI platforms operating in India." The petition alleges DeepSeek may transfer Indian users' data to Chinese servers. Sources: The420.in https://the420.in/deepseek-chinese-ai-delhi-high-court-data-privacy-case/ ; MLex (legal/regulatory news service) https://www.mlex.com/mlex/articles/2405408/deepseek-scrutiny-deepens-as-court-seeks-indian-government-s-response-to-challenge — **I found no confirmed update on this case dated after Oct 30, 2025; current (Aug 2026) status of the affidavit/licensing framework is UNVERIFIED.**

- **India's DPDP Act rules were notified Nov 14, 2025** and are, as of mid-2026, in a "soft enforcement" (guidance-only) phase; full enforcement with penalties up to ₹250 crore (₹2.5 billion) is not expected until May 13–14, 2027. Source: India Briefing, https://www.india-briefing.com/news/india-dpdp-compliance-timeline-enforcement-2026-27-44740.html/ — relevant because the cross-border-data-transfer and data-localization powers cited against DeepSeek (above) are not yet in full force.

- **Two Indian companies — Ola's Krutrim and AceCloud — began self-hosting DeepSeek's open-weight models on Indian servers** in late Jan/early Feb 2025, explicitly to keep user data resident in India. Krutrim CEO Bhavish Aggarwal said Krutrim deployed DeepSeek-R1-671B on Nvidia H100 GPUs ("first time anywhere in the world") and offered it at an introductory ₹1 per million tokens for February 2025 (later a 5-model lineup at ₹10–60 per million tokens/month); AceCloud said it ensures "data residency never exits India." Sources: OfficeChai (Feb 11, 2025) https://officechai.com/ai/ola-krutrim-makes-deepseek-available-at-introductory-pricing-of-rs-1-per-million-tokens/ ; Business Standard (Jan 31, 2025) https://www.business-standard.com/companies/news/ola-krutrim-acecloud-to-host-deepseek-s-ai-models-on-indian-servers-125013101812_1.html ; Business Standard (Feb 11, 2025) https://www.business-standard.com/companies/start-ups/krutrim-deploys-deepseek-s-r1-model-for-indian-developers-says-aggarwal-125021101894_1.html ; CIOL (Feb 3, 2025) https://www.ciol.com/news/deepseek-models-now-live-on-krutrim-cloud-driving-ai-growth-in-india-8686344 — **this is the R1/V3 generation (2025); I found no source confirming an Indian cloud provider has self-hosted V4 or V4-Flash specifically as of Aug 2026 (see Unverified).**

- **Sarvam AI (India's IndiaAI-Mission-backed model effort) closed the first tranche ($234M) of a $300M Series B in June 2026**, reaching unicorn status at ~$1.5B valuation, led by HCLTech ($150M) with Khosla Ventures and Peak XV Partners; the Indian government may end up with a 1–2% equity stake by converting compulsorily convertible debentures issued as part of an IndiaAI Mission compute subsidy (Sarvam received the largest subsidy among mission participants: ₹98.68 crore against a ₹246.71 crore compute bill for 4,096 Nvidia H100 GPUs over six months). Source: Medianama, June 2026: https://www.medianama.com/2026/06/223-govt-minority-stake-sarvam-indiaai-mission/

- **At the India AI Impact Summit (Feb 16–21, 2026, New Delhi),** Union Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw described a "frugal, sovereign and scalable" national AI strategy and announced 20,000 additional GPUs on top of an existing base of 38,000 under the IndiaAI Compute Portal; Sarvam AI launched 30B- and 105B-parameter mixture-of-experts models plus speech/vision models, and the government-backed BharatGen project launched "Param2," a 17B-parameter model covering 22 Indian languages. Source: Wikipedia, "India AI Impact Summit 2026" (cross-check against primary reporting recommended before publication): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/India_AI_Impact_Summit_2026

- **A June 2026 analysis (Bernstein, via BusinessToday) argued India has not yet had "its own DeepSeek moment"** — i.e., still lacks a globally competitive foundation model — even as Chinese models collectively processed 21.37 trillion tokens in the week ending June 21, 2026, versus 5.76 trillion for leading US models; it also cites Z.ai's GLM-5.2 as roughly one-quarter the per-token cost of comparable Anthropic models. Source: BusinessToday, June 29, 2026: https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/artificial-intelligence/story/chinas-ai-price-war-is-exposing-indias-biggest-weakness-why-india-needs-its-own-deepseek-539573-2026-06-29

## Useful context

- CoRover's **BharatGPT Mini**, pitched as "India's first offline multilingual AI model" (534M parameters, 14 Indic languages, on-device), was launched at VivaTech Paris on June 23, 2025, by Union Minister Jitin Prasada — an example of India's own small-model push distinct from Sarvam/Krutrim. No DeepSeek comparison was made in this source. Source: Inc42, June 23, 2025: https://inc42.com/buzz/corover-unveils-bharatgpt-mini-indias-first-offline-multilingual-ai-model/
- In July 2026, reports (e.g., Medianama, July 13, 2026: https://www.medianama.com/2026/07/223-government-asks-ministries-pause-deployment-openai-anthropic-ai-models/) said MeitY had told ministries to pause deploying **OpenAI and Anthropic** models for cybersecurity functions; the Press Information Bureau subsequently denied issuing any such advisory, calling the reports "fake." This concerns US models, not DeepSeek, but is relevant color: as of this research, I found no fresh, undisputed 2026 government advisory targeting Chinese AI apps specifically (the clearest sourced government action on DeepSeek specifically remains the Feb 2025 Finance Ministry circular and the ongoing Delhi HC litigation, both above).
- A comparison piece (AceCloud, undated 2026) frames Sarvam AI as "use-case-driven" (multilingual, India-specific) versus DeepSeek as "model-centric and cost-disruptive," but supplies no India-specific DeepSeek adoption/usage data: https://acecloud.ai/blog/sarvam-ai-vs-chatgpt-gemini-krutrim-deepseek/
- Forbes (Feb 23, 2026) argues Indian developers historically pick capability over sovereign-origin products (citing the failures of Koo vs. Twitter/X and Ola Maps vs. Google Maps as precedent) and that ChatGPT has 100M+ weekly active users in India — but the piece does not document actual Indian-developer migration toward DeepSeek specifically. Source: https://www.forbes.com/sites/janakirammsv/2026/02/23/indias-sovereign-ai-trap-national-pride-meets-developer-pragmatism/
- India's IT Minister said in early Feb 2025 that DeepSeek would "soon be hosted on Indian servers" for data-protection reasons — this appears to be the direct trigger for the Krutrim/AceCloud hosting deals above. Source: Business Standard, Feb 1, 2025 (same article as the download-share stat above): https://www.business-standard.com/world-news/deepseek-ai-india-global-app-store-downloads-security-concerns-125020100373_1.html
