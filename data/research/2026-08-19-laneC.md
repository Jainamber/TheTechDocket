## DPDP
- Sec 2(f)+9: child = under 18; verifiable parental consent required; absolute bar on tracking/behavioural monitoring/targeted ads at children. https://www.dpdpindia.in/dpdp-children.html ; https://ksandk.com/data-protection-and-data-privacy/child-data-protection-under-dpdp-act-parental-consent-rules/
- DPDP Rules notified Nov 13 2025 — PIB primary: https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/nov/doc20251117695301.pdf ; Rule 10(1) verifiable parental consent measures.
- Phases: Nov 13 2025 board; Nov 13 2026 enforcement powers; May 13 2027 full compliance. India Briefing https://www.india-briefing.com/news/india-dpdp-compliance-timeline-enforcement-2026-27-44740.html/ → Aug 2026 = pre-enforcement window.
- Penalty up to ₹200 crore for children's-data violations (Act max ₹250cr other category). https://ksandk.com/data-protection-and-data-privacy/penalties-adjudication-under-indias-dpdp-act-2023/
