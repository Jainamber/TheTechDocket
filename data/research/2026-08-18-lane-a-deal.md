## 1. What Nvidia committed

- **[HIGH]** Nvidia will guarantee/back up to $105 billion in financing for the **first phase** of OpenAI's Ohio data-center campus. Disclosed August 17, 2026 via an SEC Form 8-K filing plus simultaneous press releases from Nvidia, OpenAI, and SB Energy. Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-17/nvidia-to-invest-up-to-105-billion-for-openai-data-center-in-ohio) ("Nvidia Will Back First Phase of OpenAI Ohio Project With as Much as $105 Billion"); [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/17/nvidia-financing-open-ai-data-center-ohio.html); [Nvidia Newsroom](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-guarantees-sb-energy-s-ports-pike-technology-campus-in-ohio-to-exclusively-host-nvidia-ai-compute); primary SEC document: [sec.gov filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000069/sbeoainvidia-portsrelease.htm).

- **[HIGH]** Headline verbs vary across outlets for the same structure — CNBC: "Nvidia backing $105 billion in financing"; Yahoo/wire coverage: "Nvidia to provide up to $105 billion guarantee"; Bloomberg: "Nvidia to Invest Up to $105 Billion." Treat "back/finance/invest/guarantee" as journalistic paraphrase, not four different deals. Sources: [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/nvidia-invest-1-5-billion-124300582.html); [Global Banking and Finance](https://www.globalbankingandfinance.com/nvidia-provide-up-105-billion-guarantee-openais-ohio-data/); [UPI](https://www.upi.com/Top_News/US/2026/08/17/nvidia-openai-sb-energy-ports-pike-data-center-ohio/4201786985547/).

- **[MEDIUM]** The commitment is structured as a contingent guarantee, not upfront cash: reporting on the 8-K states "Nvidia's guarantee applies after specified OpenAI defaults and after recovery from a replacement lease or sale is considered." A separate outlet frames it as covering the physical asset/shell value rather than OpenAI's lease obligations directly (headline: "Nvidia's $105bn Ohio guarantee covers buildings, not OpenAI"). Source: [TheNextWeb](https://thenextweb.com/news/nvidia-openai-ports-pike-105bn-residual-value-guarantee); SEC filing (above).

- **[HIGH]** Separate from the $105B guarantee, Nvidia is making a direct $1.5 billion investment in SB Energy itself. Sources: [Nvidia Newsroom](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-guarantees-sb-energy-s-ports-pike-technology-campus-in-ohio-to-exclusively-host-nvidia-ai-compute); [StockTitan](https://www.stocktitan.net/news/NVDA/nvidia-guarantees-sb-energy-s-ports-pike-technology-campus-in-ohio-oymda2jmch95.html); CNBC (above).

- **[HIGH]** Nvidia is also named as the **exclusive** provider of the campus's core AI computing infrastructure — the site will run only Nvidia GPUs. Sources: CNBC (above); [Manila Times/GlobeNewswire release](https://www.manilatimes.net/2026/08/17/tmt-newswire/globenewswire/nvidia-guarantees-sb-energys-ports-pike-technology-campus-in-ohio-to-exclusively-host-nvidia-ai-compute/2406834).

## 2. The Ohio site

- **[HIGH]** Site name: **PORTS-Pike Technology Campus** (SEC-filing paraphrase also renders it "PORTS Technology Campus"), in Pike County, Ohio, just south of Piketon — built on the site of the former **Portsmouth Gaseous Diffusion Plant**, a Cold War-era uranium-enrichment facility. Sources: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/openai-joins-ports-pike-project/); [WOSU Public Media](https://www.wosu.org/2026-08-17/openai-joins-data-center-venture-at-former-nuclear-enrichment-site-in-pike-county); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portsmouth_Gaseous_Diffusion_Plant) (background only).

- **[HIGH]** The campus sits on a mix of private land and remediated land controlled by the U.S. Department of Energy. Sources: [DOE fact sheet](https://www.energy.gov/articles/fact-sheet-department-energy-ensuring-affordable-energy-access-ohio-while-powering-future); WOSU (above); [Ohio Tech News](https://www.ohiotechnews.com/openai-signs-on-for-pike-county-ai-campus/).

- **[HIGH]** Developer/owner/operator: **SB Energy** (SoftBank Group subsidiary) will build, own, and operate the data center; an OpenAI affiliate is the tenant under a **20-year lease**. Sources: SEC filing (above); [SB Energy](https://sbenergy.com/nvidia-ai-compute-ports-pike-ohio/); CNBC (above).

- **[HIGH]** Total planned IT/compute capacity: **8 gigawatts** — an initial **4.25 GW** (the phase Nvidia's guarantee covers) plus an option for an additional **3.75 GW**. Sources: CNBC (above); [Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/nvidia-guarantees-up-to-105b-for-8-gw-ohio-ai-campus-leased-by-openai/); [Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/markets/tech/26/08/61249540/nvidias-ohio-ai-megadeal-openai-8-gw-of-compute-and-a-1-5-billion-sb-energy-bet).

- **[MEDIUM]** Power generation to support the IT load: reporting describes up to 10 GW of new energy generation, including a specific claim that SB Energy will invest $33.3 billion (with Japanese funding) toward 9.2 GW of new natural-gas generation at the site — tied to separate coverage describing it as poised to be "the country's largest gas power plant." Sources: [Inside Climate News](https://insideclimatenews.org/news/31072026/us-largest-proposed-gas-power-plant-open-house/); [DataCenterDynamics](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/openai-in-talks-to-lease-10gw-data-center-from-sb-energy-in-ohio/) (pre-deal "talks" framing).

- **[HIGH]** Timeline: capacity is expected to come online in phases beginning in **2028**. **[MEDIUM]** Some coverage frames the overall buildout as a six-year construction project. Sources: CNBC (above); WOSU/DCD (above).

- **[MEDIUM]** Jobs claims: **35,000 construction jobs** and **2,500 permanent operating positions**. Source: [Washington Examiner](https://www.washingtonexaminer.com/policy/technology/4689837/openai-nvidia-new-ohio-data-center-largest-in-world-above-power-plant-history-ai-race/) — this precise split appeared in only one outlet in available snippets; SB Energy's own quote (Section 5) corroborates only the vaguer "tens of thousands of well-paying jobs."

- **[HIGH]** OpenAI's role is lessee/tenant (via an affiliate) of compute capacity under the 20-year lease — OpenAI is not the builder or owner of the physical campus. Sources: SEC filing (above); OpenAI (above).

- **[LOW / GAP]** No specific third-party lenders or bondholders behind the underlying debt that Nvidia's guarantee backstops were named in any result surfaced by this search set. Do not name a lender without a fresh source.

- **[MEDIUM]** Ohio community-benefit commitments: OpenAI "commits $164 million for Ohio programs" per one outlet, composed of an **$80 million** local-priorities fund (SB Energy's original $40 million commitment plus an incremental $40 million from OpenAI) and up to **$84 million** in "Codex credits" for eligible Ohio postsecondary students statewide. Source: [Ohio Tech News](https://www.ohiotechnews.com/openai-signs-on-for-pike-county-ai-campus/). See CONTESTED section for a competing "$160 million+" total.

Generic Ohio incentive programs (Job Creation Tax Credit, Enterprise Zone abatement) exist for the region but none were confirmed as applied to this project in available snippets.

- **[MEDIUM]** The U.S. Department of Energy is an active party because the site includes remediated federal land; DOE published a fact sheet framing its role as "Ensuring Affordable Energy Access in Ohio While Powering the Future of AI." Source: [energy.gov](https://www.energy.gov/articles/fact-sheet-department-energy-ensuring-affordable-energy-access-ohio-while-powering-future).
