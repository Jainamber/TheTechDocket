## 1. Alphabet's Anthropic stake

- **The 10-Q does NOT name Anthropic anywhere.** Confirmed directly from the full Q2 2026 10-Q PDF: no mention of "Anthropic" in the document at all. https://s206.q4cdn.com/479360582/files/doc_financials/2026/q2/GOOG-10-Q-Q2-2026.pdf — This is the single most important fact-check finding: every "$124B Anthropic stake" headline is sourcing the Anthropic attribution to unnamed people, not Alphabet's own disclosure.
- What the 10-Q *does* say: total non-marketable securities $131.461B as of June 30, 2026 (Q4 2025: $68.687B), of which ~$124.3B is "non-marketable equity securities accounted for under the measurement alternative," total initial **cost basis** of that measurement-alternative bucket = $47,642 million (~$47.6B). Per StockTitan's parse of the same filing: https://www.stocktitan.net/sec-filings/GOOG/10-q-alphabet-inc-quarterly-earnings-report-8ffb92bbee5d.html
- Bloomberg (2026-07-23) reported the $124.3B private-investments figure and said the filing states this value was "primarily" driven by a single investment, and that an unnamed "person familiar with the matter" identified that investment as Anthropic (info "not yet made public" by Alphabet itself). https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-23/alphabet-s-anthropic-stake-jumps-to-around-124-billion (paywalled/robots-blocked directly; content relayed via Bitget News republication: https://www.bitget.com/amp/news/detail/12560605536493) — **Key nuance: "primarily" ≠ "entirely."** The $124.3B is Alphabet's total private-company equity book, not a disclosed Anthropic-only number.
- Widely repeated 2026 figure: Anthropic stake ≈ **$124.3B–$135B** depending on which Anthropic valuation mark is used ($965B Series H mark ≈ $124–135B range; a hypothetical $1T IPO mark ≈ $140B). Fortune (Beatrice Nolan), 2026-06-04: https://fortune.com/2026/06/04/amazon-google-billions-anthropic-ipo/ ; Benzinga/Sahm Capital (same underlying piece, syndicated), 2026-06: https://www.benzinga.com/markets/ipos/26/06/52927329/alphabet-stock-offers-exposure-to-spacex-anthropic-before-ipos-what-that-means-for-investors and https://www.sahmcapital.com/news/content/alphabet-stock-offers-exposure-to-spacex-anthropic-before-ipos-what-that-means-for-investors-2026-06-02
- **Ownership percentage: ~14%, contractually capped at 15%.** Consistently repeated across 2026 sources: Fortune (2026-06-04, above) — "roughly 14% in straight equity, hard-capped contractually at 15%"; 24/7 Wall St citing Bank of America (2026-07-20) — "Alphabet holds roughly 14% of Anthropic" https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/07/20/will-anthropic-boost-alphabets-earnings-to-the-stratosphere/
- **Voting rights / board seats: none.** Original sourcing is a 2025 New York Times report (Cade Metz, Nico Grant, David McCabe), based on legal filings Anthropic submitted in a Google antitrust case, stating Google holds "no voting rights, board seats, or board observer rights" despite the 14%/15%-cap stake. Published 2025-03-12, i.e., **before** the period covered by this filing — relayed via TipRanks/The Fly: https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/google-owns-14-stake-in-anthropic-holds-no-voting-rights-ny-times-reports. Not re-confirmed in the 2026 10-Q (which doesn't name Anthropic at all) — treat the "still capped at 15%, still no vote" framing as **plausible-but-not-independently-reconfirmed for Q2 2026**, since multiple 2026 outlets keep citing ~14% (implying the cap held through the April 2026 top-up), but no 2026 primary filing restates the cap/voting terms explicitly.
- **Amount invested — figures disagree across vintages, note all:**
  - 2023: $300M (April, ~10% stake) + $2B (October, $500M upfront) = ~$2.3B total by end of 2023. Widely cited historical baseline (e.g., DataCenterDynamics 2025 reporting on the 14% stake): https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/google-owns-14-percent-of-generative-ai-business-anthropic/
  - April 2026: NEW commitment of **up to $40 billion** — $10B upfront cash + $30B performance/milestone-based, plus a 5-gigawatt, five-year Google Cloud compute commitment, done at a $350B Anthropic valuation. Yahoo Finance, 2026-04-24: https://finance.yahoo.com/sectors/technology/articles/google-investing-40-billion-anthropic-163805773.html
  - As of Q2 2026 (per Fortune, 2026-07-22): "Alphabet's Anthropic stake: $13.3 billion invested, with commitments up to $30 billion more" — https://fortune.com/2026/07/22/anthropic-spacex-investments-google-earnings-biggest-ever-profit-quarter/
  - **Disagreement flagged:** older aggregator copy (Benzinga/Sahm Capital, above) still says "Investment: $3 billion (2023–2025)" — this appears to be stale, pre-dating the April 2026 $40B commitment, and should not be used as the current "total invested" figure without the caveat. The ~$3B invested / ~$135B value ratio the user mentioned matches this **stale** pre-April-2026 baseline, not the current reported (post-top-up) figures.

## 2. Alphabet's other big equity positions / Q2 2026 10-Q

- **Non-marketable securities total: $131.461B** as of June 30, 2026, vs. $68.687B at Dec 31, 2025 (roughly 91% growth in two quarters). Source: full 10-Q via fetch, corroborated by StockTitan parse. https://s206.q4cdn.com/479360582/files/doc_financials/2026/q2/GOOG-10-Q-Q2-2026.pdf ; https://www.stocktitan.net/sec-filings/GOOG/10-q-alphabet-inc-quarterly-earnings-report-8ffb92bbee5d.html
  - Of that, ~$124.3B is under the "measurement alternative" (equity securities without readily determinable fair value — i.e., private-company stakes, "primarily" Anthropic per Bloomberg's unnamed sourcing, see §1).
  - Cost basis of that measurement-alternative bucket: $47,642 million (~$47.6B) — i.e., the *combined* cost of all such private stakes, not an Anthropic-only cost figure. 10-Q, same link.
- **SpaceX is accounted for separately from "non-marketable securities"** — because it IPO'd in June 2026, it now has a readily-determinable market price, so its stake sits elsewhere on the balance sheet: $80.0B of shares "subject to short-term restrictions" sit inside **marketable equity securities**, and $14.1B of shares "subject to long-term restrictions on the ability to sell through the third quarter of 2027" sit inside **other non-current assets** — exact 10-Q language, confirmed by direct fetch. https://s206.q4cdn.com/479360582/files/doc_financials/2026/q2/GOOG-10-Q-Q2-2026.pdf
- **Cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities: $242.474B** as of June 30, 2026 (vs. $126.843B at Dec 31, 2025 — roughly doubled, driven mainly by the SpaceX mark being folded into marketable securities post-IPO). 10-Q direct fetch, corroborated by SEC exhibit 99.1 fetch (same $242.474B figure). https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1652044/000165204426000066/googexhibit991q22026.htm
  - Breakdown: cash & equivalents $55.911B + marketable securities $186.563B = $242.474B. Same sources.
- **Gain on equity securities, Q2 2026: $99.031B** ("net unrealized gains on equity securities"); earnings release separately describes "a net gain of $98.0 billion" in other income, "primarily the result of net unrealized gains" (the $98.0B/$99.0B difference is other-income-statement-line vs. total-equity-securities-gain-line; both figures appear in the same SEC exhibit). https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1652044/000165204426000066/googexhibit991q22026.htm
- **"Other Bets" segment (Q2 2026):** revenue $382M (vs $373M Q2 2025); six-months-2026 revenue $793M (vs $823M six-months-2025, i.e. down slightly YoY); operating loss $(1.799)B for the quarter. Direct 10-Q fetch. https://s206.q4cdn.com/479360582/files/doc_financials/2026/q2/GOOG-10-Q-Q2-2026.pdf
- **Waymo is NOT part of the "non-marketable securities" line** — it's a majority/controlled subsidiary consolidated into the "Other Bets" operating segment (the $382M revenue/$1.799B operating-loss figures above), not a passive minority equity stake like SpaceX or Anthropic. Its most recent outside-money valuation is ~$110–126B: Waymo raised ~$16B in a round valuing it at ~$110B (up from >$45B in Oct 2024) — TheStreet, 2026-02: https://www.thestreet.com/investing/alphabets-quiet-110b-waymo-move-blows-up-other-bets-narrative ; a later Finviz aggregation cites Waymo touching a $126B valuation after a further fundraise: https://finviz.com/news/296661/alphabet-backed-waymos-valuation-touches-126-billion-after-latest-fundraise-to-fuel-robotaxi-expansion (secondary aggregator, treat $126B as less certain than the $110B TheStreet figure sourced to the actual raise).
- No other named private-company stakes appear in any source gathered — the "measurement alternative" bucket's other holders (beyond the "primarily" Anthropic single investment Bloomberg's source flagged) are **UNVERIFIED / not named** in any source found.

## 3. How the $94.1B SpaceX position ranks

- **SpaceX stake value: $94.1B as of June 30, 2026** — confirmed directly in the 10-Q as $80.0B (short-term restricted, in marketable securities) + $14.1B (long-term restricted through Q3 2027, in other non-current assets) = $94.1B. https://s206.q4cdn.com/479360582/files/doc_financials/2026/q2/GOOG-10-Q-Q2-2026.pdf
- **Vs. Alphabet's cash + marketable securities pile ($242.474B):** $94.1B ≈ **38.8%** of that pile (computed from the two sourced figures above; not itself a published ratio).
- **Vs. Alphabet's market cap:** stockanalysis.com puts Alphabet's market cap at **$3.99 trillion as of July 27, 2026**: https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/googl/market-cap/ — $94.1B ≈ **2.36%** of that (computed). ## Disagreements / things to hedge carefully in copy

- **Anthropic $124.3B is not an Alphabet-disclosed, Anthropic-specific figure.** It's the total private-equity ("measurement alternative") bucket in the 10-Q; Anthropic isn't named in the filing; Bloomberg's "primarily one investment = Anthropic" framing rests on an unnamed source. Any sentence flatly stating "Alphabet's Anthropic stake is worth $124.3B" should be qualified (e.g., "according to Bloomberg, citing a person familiar with the matter — Alphabet's filing itself does not name Anthropic").
- **SpaceX ownership %:** sources range 4%–7.5%/initial vs. ~6% current; 10-Q discloses dollars only, no %. Use "~6% (media estimate; not disclosed in the filing)."
- **SpaceX 2015 investment:** "$900M solo" (Motley Fool, Barchart) vs. "$1B combined with Fidelity" (AnalyticsInsight) — minor but real disagreement on whether the $900M figure is Google-only.
- **Anthropic total invested-to-date:** ranges from a stale ~$3B (pre-April-2026 aggregator copy) to $13.3B deployed (Fortune, 2026-07-22) to a $40B commitment ceiling (Yahoo Finance, 2026-04-24). These are different things (deployed vs. committed vs. stale) — do not average them or present one as "the" figure.
- **SpaceX valuation at IPO:** $1.75T (Benzinga-derived) vs. $1.77T (Fortune, tied to the confirmed $135/share IPO price per CNBC/NBC News) — use $1.77T/$135 as the better-sourced figure.
- **Waymo's $126B valuation figure** (Finviz aggregation) is less well-sourced than its $110B figure (TheStreet, tied to an actual ~$16B raise) — prefer $110B unless a firmer source for $126B turns up.
- Could not access Bloomberg's two original articles directly this session (robots.txt-disallowed both times) — all Bloomberg figures here are relayed via secondary republication (Bitget News) or via search-result titles/snippets only; flagged inline above wherever this applies.
- Could not access CNBC's Amazon $25B (2026-04-20) or Nvidia $40B (2026-05-09) articles directly (403 errors both times) — relayed via other outlets' citations of the same reporting; flagged inline above.
