## 1. Nvidia's running tally of OpenAI commitments (chronological)

- **Sept 2025** — Nvidia and OpenAI announced a strategic-partnership letter of intent: deploy "at least 10 gigawatts" of Nvidia systems; Nvidia said it "intends" to invest **up to $100 billion** in OpenAI, progressively, as each gigawatt is deployed. This was a non-binding LOI, not a signed contract. [HIGH — official: OpenAI newsroom + Nvidia newsroom] https://openai.com/index/openai-nvidia-systems-partnership/ · https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/openai-and-nvidia-announce-strategic-partnership-to-deploy-10gw-of-nvidia-systems · https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/openai-nvidia/

- **Jan 31, 2026** — Bloomberg (citing WSJ): Nvidia halted/paused the $100B investment plan. Roughly five months after the announcement, no contract had been signed and no money had changed hands; the LOI never moved from "declaratory" to "contractual." [HIGH — Bloomberg/WSJ, corroborated by CNBC below] https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-01-31/nvidia-pauses-plan-to-invest-100-billion-in-openai-wsj-says

- **Feb 3, 2026** — CNBC: deal "appears stalled," exposing a possible rift, though both companies still need each other. [HIGH — CNBC] https://www.cnbc.com/2026/02/03/nvidia-openai-stalled-on-their-mega-deal-ai-giants-need-each-other.html

- **Feb 19–27, 2026** — The $100B LOI was replaced by a direct equity stake. CNBC (Feb 19) first reported Nvidia in talks to invest up to $30B. On Feb 27, OpenAI announced a **$110 billion round at a $730 billion pre-money valuation**; Nvidia's piece was **$30 billion** (direct equity, not tied to GW-deployment milestones), alongside SoftBank ($30B) and Amazon ($50B) in the same round. The restructured deal reportedly carries no attached chip-purchase obligation. [HIGH — CNBC + Winbuzzer + Crowdfund Insider, mutually consistent] https://www.cnbc.com/2026/02/19/nvidia-is-in-talks-to-invest-up-to-30-billion-in-openai-source-says.html · https://winbuzzer.com/2026/02/22/nvidia-scales-back-openai-deal-30-billion-equity-stake-xcxwbn/ · https://www.crowdfundinsider.com/2026/02/263154-nvidia-set-to-acquire-30-billion-stake-in-openai-potentially-reshaping-ai-alliances/

- **~March 2026** — Reported that Jensen Huang confirmed the original full $100B was "probably not in the cards." [MEDIUM — single outlet in this pass] https://www.techtimes.com/articles/317839/20260605/nvidia-openai-investment-shrinks-100b-30b-compute-lock-war-continues.htm

- **July 27, 2026** — Axios: Nvidia "reignites circular AI concerns" as it weighs a fresh OpenAI financing guarantee (the seed of what becomes the Ohio deal). [MEDIUM-HIGH — Axios] https://www.axios.com/2026/07/27/nvidia-openai-financing-ai-jensen-huang-ssi

- **Pre-Aug 17, 2026 (exact date not pinned)** — WSJ reported Nvidia was proposing to backstop **up to $250 billion** for the OpenAI Ohio campus buildout; investors pushed back hard, and Nvidia scaled the guarantee back to **under $120 billion**, covering only the opening phase (~5 GW), with the rest deferred to a later decision. Backstop covers lease payments and construction-related debt on the SB Energy-built facility. [HIGH — WSJ original, picked up independently by Seeking Alpha, Yahoo Finance, Interesting Engineering, cryptobriefing, Investing.com/Reuters] https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/nvidia-scales-back-250-billion-openai-data-center-guarantee-wsj-reports-4861638 · https://seekingalpha.com/news/4633594-nvidia-scales-back-openai-data-center-guarantee-to-less-than-120b-wsj-reports · https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/nvidia-cuts-openai-ohio-data-114517517.html · https://interestingengineering.com/ai-robotics/nvidia-cuts-130b-openai-ohio-campus

## 3. The skeptic case (summary — verbatim quotes below in dedicated section)

- The core mechanical objection, per multiple finance-analysis outlets: money moves Nvidia → SB Energy (equity/financing) → SB Energy builds the data center and leases it to OpenAI → OpenAI fills it with Nvidia GPUs bought partly through financing Nvidia itself arranged. The concern is that this lets revenue get counted at multiple points in a loop that ultimately depends on one unverified input: real end-customer AI demand. [HIGH — 24/7 Wall St "wider web" piece; mechanism description also matches Advisor Perspectives/RIA "round-tripping" framing independently] https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/08/17/nvidias-circular-financing-web-gets-even-wider/ · https://www.advisorperspectives.com/articles/2025/11/10/nvidia-deals-vendor-financing-round-tripping

- **Nvidia's stock fell 5%** when the original $250B Ohio guarantee figure first surfaced, before it was trimmed. [MEDIUM — 24/7 Wall St, single outlet in this pass for this specific stat]

- **Credit default swaps (CDS) on Nvidia debt** — the cost of insuring Nvidia's debt against default — reportedly **more than doubled since late May 2026**, attributed to market anxiety over the circular-financing structure. [MEDIUM — Benzinga, single outlet in this pass] https://www.benzinga.com/markets/equities/26/07/60749765/michael-burry-warns-nvidias-overreaching-is-pushing-circular-spending-to-biblical-proportions-amid-surge-in-credit-default-swaps

- **Nvidia pushed back formally**: sent a 7-page memo to Wall Street analysts explicitly denying it engages in "vendor financing" / revenue-inflation via customer investment. Short sellers Michael Burry and Jim Chanos were reported as unconvinced. [HIGH — Advisor Perspectives on the memo; CNBC's Aug 11 headline ("Nvidia is trying to quiet 'circular financing' accusations. Wall Street is unsure it will") independently confirms the same underlying event from a second outlet] https://www.advisorperspectives.com/articles/2025/11/10/nvidia-deals-vendor-financing-round-tripping · https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/11/nvidia-tries-to-quiet-circular-financing-accusations-wall-street-unsure-it-will-.html

- **Dot-com-era parallel (Lucent/Nortel)**, repeated consistently across three independent finance-analysis outlets: Lucent lent money to customers so they could buy Lucent gear, then booked those loans as sales revenue even though repayment risk stayed on Lucent's own books. Nortel lent more than **$7 billion** to startup telecom carriers to buy Nortel equipment — many loans interest-free, unsecured, and tied to future purchases. When the telecom bubble burst, Nortel's customers failed and dragged Nortel into bankruptcy: stock peaked at **$86.75 in July 2000**, was worth **$0.18 by 2009**. One source frames Nortel's collapse as "$398 billion to zero in under a decade" (this specific market-cap figure was not pinned to one single named outlet in my search results — treat the $398B number as MEDIUM/plausible-but-not-firmly-sourced; the $86.75→$0.18 share-price collapse is corroborated across multiple sources and is HIGH). [HIGH for the mechanism + share-price collapse; MEDIUM for the exact "$398B" figure] https://www.advisorperspectives.com/articles/2025/11/10/nvidia-deals-vendor-financing-round-tripping · https://realinvestmentadvice.com/resources/blog/nvidia-deals-round-tripping-or-vendor-financing/ · https://tomtunguz.com/nvidia_nortel_vendor_financing_comparison/
