## 5. Explainer needs (financial literacy for readers)

- **"Backstop" / financing guarantee, plain English:** a backstop is a financial commitment where a well-capitalized company (here, Nvidia) pledges to stand behind a customer's debt or lease obligations, which lets that customer (OpenAI, via SB Energy's project financing) borrow at better terms than its own credit alone would support — because lenders are really underwriting Nvidia's balance sheet, not just OpenAI's. It behaves like a debt obligation on the guarantor's own books even if the customer never misses a payment. [HIGH — consistent description across Barchart, MLQ News, and TechTimes] https://www.barchart.com/story/news/3551625/nvidias-openai-backstop-puts-ai-financing-risk-in-focus , https://mlq.ai/news/nvidia-in-talks-to-backstop-250-billion-in-openai-data-center-financing/
- **"Vendor financing," plain English:** when a supplier (Nvidia) provides capital, credit guarantees, or equity to its own customers (OpenAI, neoclouds, etc.) so those customers can keep buying the supplier's product — a long-standing practice in capital-intensive industries, not unique to AI. [HIGH — described consistently by Axios and the Bloomberg circular-deals graphic] https://www.axios.com/2026/07/27/nvidia-openai-financing-ai-jensen-huang-ssi , https://www.bloomberg.com/graphics/2026-ai-circular-deals/
- **"Circular financing," plain English:** the specific concern that arises when the vendor-financing loop closes — Nvidia funds a customer, the customer uses that funding (directly or via debt Nvidia backstops) to buy Nvidia chips, and the resulting "demand" and revenue look organic even though Nvidia's own capital seeded it. Critics say this can inflate the appearance of real end-market demand; defenders call it an efficient "virtuous circle" for capital-intensive buildouts. Analyses cited put the broader circular-deal web at **over $800 billion** across the AI industry as of 2026. [HIGH for the definition (corroborated by Axios, Benzinga, TradingKey, Acadian, Bloomberg's graphic); MEDIUM for the specific "$800 billion" tally, which appeared in only one synthesized result] https://www.tradingkey.com/analysis/stocks/us-stocks/262068483-nvidia-skhynix-openai-750-billion-deal-cyclical-financing-market-panic-tradingkey , https://www.acadian-asset.com/investment-insights/owenomics/straight-talk-about-circular-deals-in-ai
