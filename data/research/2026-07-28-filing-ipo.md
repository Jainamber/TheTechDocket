## 1. The disclosure itself (Alphabet Q2 2026)

- Alphabet's Q2 2026 quarterly SEC filing discloses a **$94.1 billion** stake in Space Exploration Technologies Corp. (SpaceX) as of **June 30, 2026**. — Bloomberg: https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-23/google-says-it-holds-94-billion-in-spacex-shares-after-ipo ; Motley Fool: https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/07/23/alphabet-just-revealed-a-94-billion-stake-in-space/ ; Yahoo Finance: https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/alphabet-just-revealed-94-billion-032600195.html ; Yahoo Finance (Google reports): https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/google-reports-94-billion-stake-122126618.html
- Coverage broke on **July 22–23, 2026**, immediately after Alphabet's Q2 earnings release (dated July 22, 2026 per SEC 8-K, see §4). Some outlets label the filing date July 22, others July 23 — I could not pull the exact 10-Q accession number/timestamp directly from SEC EDGAR (see gap note below), so treat "July 22-23, 2026" as the verified window rather than a single verified date.
- **Accounting classification** — split into two buckets, per language that stocktitan.net (an SEC-filings aggregator) quotes directly from the 10-Q: "Includes $80.0 billion of Space Exploration Technologies Corp. (SpaceX) shares subject to short-term restrictions on the ability to sell" (classified as **marketable equity securities**) and "$14.1 billion of SpaceX shares subject to long-term restrictions on the ability to sell through the third quarter of 2027" (classified in **other non-current assets**). — https://www.stocktitan.net/sec-filings/GOOG/10-q-alphabet-inc-quarterly-earnings-report-8ffb92bbee5d.html
  - NOTE: I attempted to reach Alphabet's actual 10-Q document directly on SEC EDGAR (CIK 0001652044) multiple times; I could confirm CIK, the 10-K (filed Feb 5, 2026), and the Q2 earnings 8-K (accession 0001652044-26-000066, filed July 22, 2026, exhibit 99.1 = press release, confirmed via https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1652044/000165204426000066/0001652044-26-000066-index.htm ), but not the standalone 10-Q accession/URL itself. The $80.0B/$14.1B split and its "marketable equity securities" framing is corroborated by multiple secondary outlets (see below) but the verbatim footnote text above should be treated as filing-adjacent (via aggregator), not hand-confirmed on sec.gov by me.
- **Percentage stake — outlets disagree, both figures reported:**
  - **~6%**: used in Motley Fool's July 27 headline/analysis (https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/07/27/googl-stake-spacex-941-billion-anthropic-even-more/), Teslarati (https://www.teslarati.com/google-massive-stake-spacex-will-shock-you/), and TradingKey, which frames it as "Google's stake end of 2025: about 6%" (https://www.tradingkey.com/analysis/stocks/us-stocks/262049922-alphabet-google-holds-94-billion-spacex-shares-hundredfold-tradingkey).
  - **~4.9–5%** (current, post-dilution): Yahoo Finance/Motley Fool July 23 piece ("approximately 4.9% of SpaceX (down from ~6% before recent dilution)" — https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/07/23/alphabet-just-revealed-a-94-billion-stake-in-space/ ), Seeking Alpha (https://seekingalpha.com/news/4617321-google-owns-94b-in-spacex-shares-after-historic-ipo), BigGo Finance (https://finance.biggo.com/news/951fc8e1-5e9e-4b46-a537-d0892ddcecd7), American Bazaar ("Google now owns 5% of SpaceX" — https://americanbazaaronline.com/2026/07/23/google-now-owns-5-of-spacex-after-historic-ipo-485135/), and Motley Fool's own July 3, 2026 piece ("Current stake: 4.9%" — https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/07/03/googl-invested-900-million-spcx-how-much-today/).
  - **Explanation for the gap**: multiple sources tie the drop from ~6% to ~4.9% to dilution from SpaceX's **February 2026 merger/combination with xAI** at a combined $1.25 trillion valuation (see §3). — TradingKey (as above); americanbazaaronline (as above, "dilution... corporate transactions involving SpaceX and xAI").
  - Conclusion for the writer: **~6% is the pre-dilution (end-2025) figure; ~4.9-5% is the more precisely sourced current/post-IPO figure.** The task brief's "about 6%" appears to reflect the looser/older figure that several outlets still use as shorthand.
- **Alphabet's overall market cap context**: the $94.1B SpaceX stake is roughly **2.4%** of Alphabet's own ~**$3.9 trillion** market capitalization. — Yahoo Finance: https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/alphabet-just-revealed-94-billion-032600195.html
- **Lock-up mechanics**: the $80B "short-term restricted" tranche begins releasing on **August 6, 2026** — two trading days after SpaceX's first earnings report as a public company, scheduled for **August 4, 2026**. The $14.1B tranche stays locked through **Q3 2027**. — TradingKey (as above); BigGo Finance (as above); Yahoo/Fool: https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/alphabet-just-revealed-94-billion-032600195.html

## 2. SpaceX IPO — date, price, exchange, valuation, proceeds

- **IPO/pricing date**: priced June 11, 2026; **trading commenced Friday, June 12, 2026**. — Official SpaceX pricing announcement (primary source): https://content.spacex.com/cms-assets/FINAL_Documents%20and%20Updates/SpaceX_PricingAnnouncement.pdf ; corroborated by TechCrunch: https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/12/spacex-ipo-closes-up-19-and-delivers-the-worlds-first-trillionaire/ ; NBC News: https://www.nbcnews.com/business/markets/spacex-ipo-stock-price-rcna349760 ; Forbes live updates: https://www.forbes.com/sites/tylerroush/2026/06/12/spacex-opens-at-150-surging-17-after-largest-ipo-ever-live-updates/
- **Ticker/exchange**: **SPCX** on **Nasdaq Global Select Market**, dual-listed on **Nasdaq Texas**. — Official pricing announcement (as above); IPO Club: https://www.ipo.club/deals/spacex
- **Offer price: $135.00 per share.** — Official SpaceX pricing announcement (primary): as above; confirmed by NBC News, TechCrunch, Forbes, IPO Club (all as above).
- **Base offering size**: 555,555,555 Class A shares at $135 = base proceeds of ~**$75.0 billion**, plus underwriters' 30-day option for an additional 83,333,333 shares. — Official pricing announcement (primary, as above); IPO Club (as above).
- **Total raised after full greenshoe exercise: ~$85.7 billion** (638,888,888 total shares sold), closing June 15, 2026 — confirmed via SpaceX's own investor-relations release: "The issuance of all shares closed on June 15, 2026, bringing the gross proceeds from the initial public offering to SpaceX to approximately $85.7 billion" — https://ir.spacex.com/updates/releases-details/2026/Space-Exploration-Technologies-Corp--Announces-Closing-of-Initial-Public-Offering-Including-Full-Exercise-of-Underwriters-Option-to-Purchase-Additional-Shares-2026-RgoR-Y1Vwh/default.aspx . Also reported by CNBC: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/15/spacex-ipo-spcx-greenshoe-overallotment.html and Yahoo Finance: https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/spacex-ipo-raises-85-7-142359726.html — this is the largest IPO in history per multiple outlets (TechCrunch, Forbes, IPO Club, as above).
- **First-day trading**: opened at **$150/share**; closed around **$160.95–$161** (~19% above the $135 offer price); intraday high **$176** (TechCrunch) on day one. — TechCrunch (as above); Forbes (as above); CNBC: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/12/spacex-ipo-spcx-live-updates.html (headline states "SPCX closes at $161, jumping 19% after record debut").
- **Valuation figures — some spread across sources, all reported here rather than reconciled by me:**

  - ~$1.75 trillion "near" — IPO Club (as above).
  - Market cap **$2.1 trillion** at opening ($150) per Forbes (as above); TechCrunch cites an intraday peak market cap of **$2.3 trillion** at the $160.95 close. These two figures come from different outlets using different price points/share-count bases — flagging as an unresolved cross-source discrepancy, not something I can adjudicate without the underlying share count.
  - Pre-IPO reporting had targeted a **$1.5 trillion** valuation (FT, via Yahoo: https://finance.yahoo.com/news/spacex-weighs-june-2026-ipo-050656741.html) and separately **"more than $2 trillion"** per a Bloomberg report (via aol.com mirror: https://www.aol.com/articles/spacex-targets-more-2-trillion-195553274.html) — both pre-pricing estimates, included for context on how the eventual $135/$1.77T pricing compares to prior targets.

- **Elon Musk became "the world's first trillionaire"** on the debut, with an estimated fortune of $1.1 trillion, per Forbes and TechCrunch (both as above). Musk quote (Forbes): "It is certainly hard to believe that little company that started in a warehouse in El Segundo is now going public."

I only found the $225.64 peak figure repeated across Motley-Fool-linked coverage; I did not independently confirm it against a primary price-data source, so treat the exact peak as **weakly sourced** (plausible, not independently corroborated).

## 3. History of Google/Alphabet's SpaceX investment

- **Contemporaneous January 2015 reporting (four independent outlets, consistent with each other):** Google and Fidelity together invested **$1 billion** in SpaceX for a **combined stake "just under 10%"** (Forbes: "just under 10%"; TechCrunch: "just shy of 10 percent"; GeekWire: "just under 10%"; Bloomberg: "almost 10 percent"), valuing SpaceX at **"more than $10 billion."**
  - Forbes (Jan 20, 2015): https://www.forbes.com/sites/alexknapp/2015/01/20/spacex-raises-1-billion-in-a-financing-round-with-google-and-fidelity/
  - TechCrunch (Jan 20, 2015): https://techcrunch.com/2015/01/20/spacex-raises-1-billion-in-new-funding-from-google-and-fidelity/
  - GeekWire (2015): https://www.geekwire.com/2015/spacex-lands-1b-investment-google-fidelity-helping-fuel-seattle-based-satellite-venture/
  - Bloomberg (Jan 20, 2015): https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2015-01-20/musk-s-spacex-sells-10-stake-to-google-fidelity-for-1-billion
- **Conflicting 2026 retrospective framing**: Motley Fool's 2026 coverage (analyst Sean Williams) breaks the round down differently, saying **Google alone put in $900 million** for a **~7.5% stake**, valuing SpaceX at **~$12 billion** at the time — this is Google's individual share of the round in Motley Fool's telling, vs. the "combined $1B / ~10%" framing used contemporaneously in 2015. — Motley Fool (July 3, 2026): https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/07/03/googl-invested-900-million-spcx-how-much-today/ ; repeated in Motley Fool's July 27 piece (https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/07/27/googl-stake-spacex-941-billion-anthropic-even-more/) and in TradingKey and crypto.news (both as above).
  - **UNVERIFIED / not reconciled**: I could not find a primary document (e.g., SpaceX's S-1 beneficial-ownership tables) breaking out Google's individual dollar contribution and share separately from Fidelity's in the 2015 round. The "$900M/7.5%/$12B" figures and the "$1B combined/~10%/~$10B" figures are not necessarily contradictory (Google could have supplied the bulk of the round with Fidelity taking a smaller slice), but I was not able to confirm the exact split against a primary source, so both framings are presented here rather than merged.
- **Return framing**: Motley Fool/crypto.news calculate a **12,156% return over 11.5 years** on the original $900M, valuing the stake at **$110.3 billion** as of July 3, 2026 (before the $94.1B figure was disclosed for June 30, 2026 — note the $110.3B figure is from a later date, July 3, and is higher than the June 30 mark of $94.1B, consistent with SPCX's price having moved between those dates). — https://crypto.news/alphabets-billion-dollars-spacex-gamble-balloons/ . TradingKey separately frames it as "~100-fold return" in its headline: https://www.tradingkey.com/analysis/stocks/us-stocks/262049922-alphabet-google-holds-94-billion-spacex-shares-hundredfold-tradingkey
- **Interim mark-to-market history (pre-IPO)**: Alphabet had already been marking gains on this stake before the IPO. Bloomberg reported that Alphabet's **Q1 2025** profit got an **"$8 billion boost... from unrealized gains from an investment in a private company"** (unnamed in the article's snippet but consistent with SpaceX given context), with SpaceX valued at roughly **$350 billion** as of a **December 2024** transaction. — https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-04-24/alphabet-s-profit-gets-8-billion-boost-from-spacex-investment (Note: article dated April 24, 2025; my fetch tool's summary said "Q1 ended March 2024," which is inconsistent with the article's own April 2025 publish date — flagging this as likely a summarization slip on a fetched intermediate source rather than a verified fact; the safer verified claim is simply "Alphabet reported an $8 billion unrealized gain tied to this stake in a Q1 report around April 2025," which I could not independently pin to a specific quarter-end date beyond that.)
- **SpaceX-xAI merger (relevant to the stake-percentage dilution in §1)**: SpaceX and xAI (Musk's AI company) combined in **February 2026** at a **combined $1.25 trillion valuation**, ahead of the IPO. — Bloomberg: https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-02-02/elon-musk-s-spacex-said-to-combine-with-xai-ahead-of-mega-ipo ; CNBC: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/02/03/musk-xai-spacex-biggest-merger-ever.html ; Motley Fool: https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/03/31/spacex-absorbed-xai-at-a-combined-125-trillion-val/

- Multiple outlets frame this explicitly as a **non-cash, mark-to-market accounting event**: "this unrealized gain did not provide actual cash — the company 'adjusted the value of the holdings on its balance sheet without selling the shares.'" — Analytics Insight: https://www.analyticsinsight.net/news/alphabet-holds-941-billion-in-spacex-shares-under-full-trading-lock-up

TradingKey separately cites Anthropic's private valuation jumping "from $350 billion to $965 billion in Q2 2026." — Motley Fool: https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/07/27/googl-stake-spacex-941-billion-anthropic-even-more/ ; TradingKey (as above).
