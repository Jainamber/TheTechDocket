vendor-published, and at least one outlet (apidog.com) explicitly flagged that "no independent
evaluations existed" for Qwen3.8-Max at time of publication.** Independent/crowdsourced
leaderboard placement (Arena.AI) is a separate, more independent data source — use it, not the
vendor table, when a claim needs third-party backing.
