## 1. The Nvidia–OpenAI $105B Ohio Deal (~Aug 17–18, 2026)

- Nvidia agreed to back **up to $105 billion in financing** for a new OpenAI-leased data center campus in **Pike County, Ohio**, announced **Monday Aug 17, 2026**. **VERIFIED** — reported by CNBC, Bloomberg, Crain's Cleveland, Axios, Yahoo Finance/Bloomberg mirror. (source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/17/nvidia-financing-open-ai-data-center-ohio.html) (source: https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-17/nvidia-to-invest-up-to-105-billion-for-openai-data-center-in-ohio) (source: https://www.crainscleveland.com/technology/ccl-nvidia-investment-ohio-data-center-20260817/) (source: https://www.axios.com/2026/08/17/openai-nvidia-ohio-data-center-sb-energy)
- OpenAI signed a **20-year lease**. Site capacity: **up to ~8 gigawatts of IT computing capacity**, to be powered by **10 gigawatts of new energy generation**; first **800 megawatts** of capacity expected online by **2028**. Site sits partly on federal land formerly used for uranium enrichment. **VERIFIED** (source: https://www.axios.com/2026/08/17/openai-nvidia-ohio-data-center-sb-energy) (source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/17/nvidia-financing-open-ai-data-center-ohio.html)
- Facility will be **built and owned by SoftBank's SB Energy**; **Nvidia will be the exclusive chip provider** to the site. Nvidia is also separately investing **$1.5 billion directly in SB Energy**. OpenAI claims the project will create **35,000 construction jobs** and **2,500 permanent operating jobs**. **VERIFIED** (source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/17/nvidia-financing-open-ai-data-center-ohio.html) (source: https://www.crainscleveland.com/technology/ccl-nvidia-investment-ohio-data-center-20260817/)
- **Deal evolution — this is the sharpest tension fact for the story.** The $105B guarantee is materially smaller than what had been reported earlier: WSJ reporting in July had Nvidia weighing a guarantee of **up to $250 billion**, which itself triggered a ~4.5% intraday NVDA share drop on "circular financing" concerns. WSJ then reported **Aug 14, 2026** that the number had already been cut to **"less than $120 billion."** The two sides landed at **$105B when signed Aug 17** — i.e., the final commitment came in **$145 billion lower** than the figure reported roughly a month earlier. **VERIFIED**, multiply corroborated. (source: https://fortune.com/2026/08/18/openai-data-center-deal-with-nvidia-comes-in-145-billion-lower-than-reportedsignaling-concerns-of-artificial-demand-for-chips/) (source: https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/openai-data-center-deal-nvidia-170852785.html) (source: https://www.dagens.com/technology/nvidia-scales-back-massive-openai-deal-amid-fears-of-an-ai-money-merry-go-round)
- Analyst framing: the scale-down is read as Nvidia limiting its own exposure amid "circular financing" concerns (Nvidia money flowing to OpenAI, which uses it to buy Nvidia chips, propping up both companies' numbers) and questions about whether AI chip demand is being artificially inflated by this financing loop. CNBC's Aug 18 analysis frames Nvidia's OpenAI relationship as shifting "from chips to capital." **VERIFIED** (source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/18/nvidias-ai-moat-is-shifting-from-chips-to-capital.html) (source: https://fortune.com/2026/08/18/openai-data-center-deal-with-nvidia-comes-in-145-billion-lower-than-reportedsignaling-concerns-of-artificial-demand-for-chips/)

**Tension note for the writer:** OpenAI signed an up-to-$105B, 20-year, 8-gigawatt infrastructure commitment on Aug 17 — the day before (per TIME's Aug 18 exclusive) Altman told the world OpenAI is deliberately slowing frontier training for safety reasons. The compute buildout is about long-run capacity (inference + future training runs across years), not necessarily today's frontier run, but the juxtaposition of dates is the story's core tension and is well-documented.

## 4. Rival Safety Postures + Reactions

**Anthropic — Responsible Scaling Policy (RSP) v3.0**
- Updated **Feb 24, 2026**. Official pages: **VERIFIED** (source: https://www.anthropic.com/responsible-scaling-policy) (source: https://anthropic.com/news/responsible-scaling-policy-v3)
- Key substantive change: Anthropic **removed the original "hard pause" trigger** (the prior commitment to pause scaling/deployment if safety-procedure compliance couldn't keep up with capability at a given ASL). It replaced this with a **tiered system built around ASL-3 Security Standards and a public "Frontier Safety Roadmap,"** scaling security requirements proportionally to capability rather than making pause/deploy a binary decision. Anthropic states it remains free to pause "in any circumstances [it deems] appropriate" even where not RSP-mandated. **VERIFIED**, analysis from a credible AI-governance research org. (source: https://www.governance.ai/analysis/anthropics-rsp-v3-0-how-it-works-whats-changed-and-some-reflections) — additional commentary/corroboration, **SINGLE-SOURCE** tier: (source: https://thezvi.substack.com/p/anthropic-responsible-scaling-policy)

**Google DeepMind — Frontier Safety Framework (FSF) v3.0**
- Official: **VERIFIED** (source: https://deepmind.google/blog/strengthening-our-frontier-safety-framework/)
- **April 17, 2026**: added **"Tracked Capability Levels" (TCLs)**, an early-warning layer to spot less-extreme risks sooner, plus a new **"Harmful Manipulation" Critical Capability Level (CCL)** covering AI systems capable of systematically altering human beliefs.
- Framework spans four risk domains: **CBRN** (chemical/biological/radiological/nuclear), **cybersecurity**, **ML R&D** (i.e., recursive self-improvement-type risk), and **deceptive alignment**.
- **June 18, 2026**: DeepMind added an **"AI Control Roadmap"** — a system-level framework for securing advanced agents deployed inside Google, treating agents as potential insider threats and layering monitoring/intervention on top of alignment work.

**Meta — Frontier AI / Advanced AI Scaling Framework**
- Official: **VERIFIED** (source: https://ai.meta.com/static-resource/Meta_Advanced-AI-Scaling-Framework-v2) — "Advanced AI Scaling Framework Version 2"
- Assigns models to **Moderate / High / Critical** risk thresholds based on potential to enable catastrophic outcomes in the deployment context in question. If a model is assessed at the **Critical** threshold and risk **cannot be mitigated, Meta says it will stop development.**

**xAI — Risk Management Framework**
- Official PDF, dated **Dec 31, 2025**: **VERIFIED** (source: https://data.x.ai/2025-12-31-xai-frontier-artificial-intelligence-framework.pdf)
- Covers two risk categories: **malicious use** and **loss of control**; xAI's framework leans more heavily on **quantitative benchmarks/thresholds** than peers — e.g., a malicious-use eval built with **SecureBio** requiring an answer rate of **less than 1-in-20** on restricted queries as a deployment gate.

## 5. Historical Framing

**March 2023 — FLI "Pause Giant AI Experiments" open letter**
- Published by the **Future of Life Institute**, **March 2023** (this pass corroborates "March 2023" consistently; could not independently re-verify the specific day-of-month — commonly cited elsewhere as March 22 — from primary sourcing in this search pass, so treat day-level precision as **unconfirmed** if needed for print). **VERIFIED** at the month level. (source: https://futureoflife.org/open-letter/pause-giant-ai-experiments/) (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pause_Giant_AI_Experiments:_An_Open_Letter)
- Asked **"all AI labs to immediately pause for at least 6 months the training of AI systems more powerful than GPT-4,"** citing risks including AI-generated propaganda, mass automation of jobs, "human obsolescence," and loss of societal control. **VERIFIED**
- Gathered **30,000+ signatures**. Signatories consistently corroborated across sources (Wikipedia, SSRN academic response paper, NY1 news coverage from the time): **Yoshua Bengio, Stuart Russell, Elon Musk, Steve Wozniak, Yuval Noah Harari**, Stability AI CEO **Emad Mostaque**, and NYU professor **Gary Marcus**. **VERIFIED** (source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pause_Giant_AI_Experiments:_An_Open_Letter) (source: https://ny1.com/nyc/all-boroughs/news/2023/03/29/musk-wozniak-tech-artificial-intelligence-ai) (source: https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4412516)
- **Note on a discarded claim**: one search result surfaced a list also naming Prince Harry, Meghan, and Steve Bannon as signatories. This could not be corroborated by any other source in this pass and looks like corrupted/vandalized content rather than fact — **excluded, do not use.**
- **Outcome**: no pause occurred; GPT-4-class and successor development continued industry-wide. The letter is best used as "researcher/external-advocacy pressure that didn't change lab behavior" — a contrast case to this week's news, where (per OpenAI's own account) the pause originates from inside the lab.

**The modern analogue — "Pacing the Frontier" letter, July 2026**
- Published **~July 28, 2026**, signed by **1,100+ employees** (different outlets report slightly different counts, ranging roughly **1,100–1,200+** — treat exact headcount as approximate/rounded across outlets) across **OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind, and Meta**, including some chief scientists and cofounders. **VERIFIED** core facts, multi-source. (source: https://fortune.com/2026/07/29/anthropic-deepmind-openai-meta-washington-ai-slowdown-plan/) (source: https://www.cnn.com/2026/07/28/tech/ai-development-tech-employees-open-letter) (source: https://thenextweb.com/news/pacing-the-frontier-ai-employees-letter-us-government) (source: https://abc7ny.com/story/employees-worlds-biggest-ai-companies-are-calling-slowdown-development/19596454/)
- Unlike the 2023 FLI letter, signatories explicitly say they are **not** calling for an immediate pause — they're asking the **US government** to help build the **technical and governance tools for a coordinated pause if later needed**, citing "a real risk that capability development rapidly accelerates beyond our ability to understand or control the resulting systems," with **recursive self-improvement** (AI accelerating its own development) named as the specific worry. **VERIFIED** (source: https://fortune.com/2026/07/29/anthropic-deepmind-openai-meta-washington-ai-slowdown-plan/)
- This is the more relevant "prior instance" for the story: it's the immediate precedent showing OpenAI and Anthropic both moving, on the record, toward slower/more-coordinated frontier development in the month before Altman's TIME interview — i.e., this week's news reads as a continuation of a trend already underway since late July, not a bolt from nowhere.
