## STORY 2 — Meta youth-harm trial opens in Oakland
**Anchor:** https://www.nbcnews.com/business/corporations/meta-trial-facebook-instagram-begins-rcna593146

- Trial began **Tuesday, August 18, 2026**, in federal court in Oakland, California, with **U.S. District Judge Yvonne Gonzalez Rogers** presiding (source: https://www.cnn.com/2026/08/18/tech/meta-attorneys-general-addiction-trial-opening-arguments; corroborated by https://oaklandside.org/2026/08/18/meta-trial-claiming-platforms-addicted-children-begins-oakland/ and https://abc7news.com/post/opening-arguments-begin-oakland-metas-federal-trial-facebook-instagram-child-safety/19697124/).
- Case brought by a bipartisan coalition of **29 state attorneys general**, with California, Colorado, Kentucky, and New Jersey as the four lead states presenting the case at trial (source: https://www.usnews.com/news/top-news/articles/2026-08-18/meta-faces-29-state-trial-that-could-reshape-instagram-and-facebook; corroborated by https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/17/meta-attorneys-general-california-federal-trial-astronomical-consequences.html and https://www.technology.org/2026/08/19/meta-29-state-trial-instagram-facebook-teen-design/).
- Core allegation: Meta intentionally designed Facebook and Instagram features to addict young users and harm their mental health (anxiety, depression, and in some cases suicide cited), while misleading the public/consumers about platform safety; suit also alleges Meta violated COPPA by knowingly collecting data from children under 13 without parental consent (source: https://www.nbcnews.com/business/corporations/meta-trial-facebook-instagram-begins-rcna593146; corroborated by https://oaklandside.org/2026/08/18/meta-trial-claiming-platforms-addicted-children-begins-oakland/).
- Trial is expected to run roughly six to seven weeks; an eight-person jury will issue an advisory verdict, but Judge Gonzalez Rogers will determine Meta's liability (source: aggregated from https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/17/meta-attorneys-general-california-federal-trial-astronomical-consequences.html).
- **Remedy sought:** Attorneys general had not specified a final penalty ask as of the article, but said at a pretrial hearing the amount could be "closer to **$200 billion**" — equivalent to roughly three years of Meta's after-tax profit (source: https://www.nbcnews.com/business/corporations/meta-trial-facebook-instagram-begins-rcna593146).
- **Separately**, a $1.4 trillion figure has circulated in headlines as the amount "at stake" (source: https://www.pressdemocrat.com/2026/08/18/meta-trial-harms-facebook-instagram-california-children-oakland-federal-court/), but California AG Rob Bonta explicitly told the court "we are not asking for $1.4 trillion," calling it "the highest possible number that could ever be calculated" and describing the case as being about restitution, not simple damages (source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/18/meta-attorneys-general-opening-arguments-california-bonta-restitution.html). **RED FLAG: do not report $1.4T as the ask — plaintiffs' own counsel disclaims it.**
- Meta's stated defense: Meta lawyer Paul Schmidt argued in opening statements that the states' case misrepresents Meta's platforms and policies, accused the attorneys general of cherry-picking internal documents, and argued there is genuine scientific disagreement over whether "social media addiction" is a real, definable condition (source: https://www.yahoo.com/news/articles/social-media-addicting-brains-children-032334098.html). Headline framing elsewhere: "Meta Rejects Allegations It Sought to Hook Children" (source: https://www.usnews.com/news/top-news/articles/2026-08-18/meta-faces-29-state-trial-that-could-reshape-instagram-and-facebook).

**STATUS: VERIFIED (2+ outlets)** across all core facts — trial date/court/judge, plaintiff count, allegation, and Meta's defense gist are consistently corroborated by NBC (anchor), CNN, CNBC, ABC7, Oaklandside, NPR, and US News. The dollar figures need care (see red flag above).

## STORY 4 — Apple ImageIO security patch (CVE-2026-65346)
**Anchor:** https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/08/18/apple-plugs-image-processing-hole-ripe-for-spyware-abuse/5289031

- **CVE-2026-65346** is an integer-overflow vulnerability in **ImageIO**, Apple's framework for reading/writing image data, which could allow arbitrary code execution when a device processes a maliciously crafted image (source: https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/08/18/apple-plugs-image-processing-hole-ripe-for-spyware-abuse/5289031; corroborated by https://www.brinztech.com/breach-alerts/brinztech-alert-apple-issues-critical-security-updates-to-patch-imageio-integer-overflow-vulnerability-cve-2026-65346).
- Fixed in updates released **August 17, 2026**: **iOS 26.6.1**, iPadOS 26.6.1, **macOS Tahoe 26.6.2**, and visionOS 26.6.1, plus **iOS 18.7.10** and iPadOS 18.7.10 for older devices (source: https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/17/apple-releases-ios-26-6-1-and-more/; corroborated by https://appleworld.today/2026/08/apple-posts-ios-26-6-1-ipados-26-6-1-macos-26-6-2-and-visionos-26-6-1-with-security-fixes/).
- watchOS and tvOS were **not** updated in this round — Apple's own security-releases index still lists both at version 26.6, implying they weren't patched/affected this time (source: https://support.apple.com/en-us/100100).
- iOS 26.6.1 patches "29 security vulnerabilities" total per one outlet, including the ImageIO flaw and a Telephony bug allowing IPSec authentication bypass (source: https://finance.biggo.com/news/f189ab75-50e2-4e87-be72-d9fa71225c74); 9to5Mac separately describes it as fixing "20+ security issues," mostly WebKit plus Audio/ImageIO/Kernel items (source: https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/17/ios-26-6-1-has-fixes-for-20-security-issues-on-iphone-details-here/) — same ballpark, minor count variance by outlet.
- **Apple has not said the ImageIO flaw was exploited in the wild** (source: https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/08/18/apple-plugs-image-processing-hole-ripe-for-spyware-abuse/5289031). Coverage frames the risk as similar to prior zero-click spyware delivery vectors (e.g., via WhatsApp/iMessage image receipt) without claiming this specific bug was used that way (source: same anchor article, corroborated by https://www.bitdefender.com/en-us/blog/hotforsecurity/update-iphone-mac-image-flaw-spyware-potential).
- Reported by **Nik Tsytsarkin of Meta's Red Team X** (source: Apple's own security-content advisory page, https://support.apple.com/en-us/148281 — this is the standard location Apple credits researchers; flagged as slightly lower-confidence attribution since it wasn't quoted verbatim in a search snippet, but matches Apple's advisory-page format).
- "Update your devices" framing: users on **iPhone (iOS 26.6.1 or, for older/unsupported-for-26 devices, iOS 18.7.10)**, **iPad (iPadOS 26.6.1 / 18.7.10)**, **Mac (macOS Tahoe 26.6.2)**, and **Vision Pro (visionOS 26.6.1)** should update; no separate Apple Watch or Apple TV update was issued for this round (source: https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/17/apple-releases-ios-26-6-1-and-more/ and https://support.apple.com/en-us/100100).

**STATUS: VERIFIED (2+ outlets)** — CVE number, affected component, fixed-in versions, release date, and "not known exploited" are all consistently corroborated across The Register (anchor), MacRumors, 9to5Mac, Malwarebytes, BigGo Finance, and Apple's own support page.
