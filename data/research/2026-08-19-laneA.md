# Lane A — Launch facts (ChatGPT for Teens, Aug 18 2026)

## Announcement
- "ChatGPT for Teens" announced Tue Aug 18, 2026; dedicated experience for 13–17, a mode/account state, not a separate app. Corroborated: TechCrunch https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/18/openai-launches-a-safer-chatgpt-for-teens-years-after-teens-started-using-it/ ; CNBC https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/18/openai-chatgpt-for-teens-safety.html ; Axios https://www.axios.com/2026/08/18/openai-chatgpt-for-teens ; CNN https://www.cnn.com/2026/08/18/tech/openai-chatgpt-for-teens ; US News https://www.usnews.com/news/technology/articles/2026-08-18/openai-launches-chatgpt-for-teens-promising-a-more-age-appropriate-chatbot
- Blocks/limits: romantic/sexual role-play, graphic violence, viral challenges, extreme beauty ideals; model barred from implying feelings/consciousness toward user. Self-harm prompts → human review team may notify linked parent. Fortune https://fortune.com/2026/08/18/openai-chatgpt-teens-age-assurance-safety/ ; NBC Bay Area https://www.nbcbayarea.com/news/national-international/openai-launches-chatgpt-for-teens-promising-age-appropriate-chatbot/4129564/
- Study Mode: guiding questions/scaffolding not direct answers; "homework nudge" on likely cheating; parents/teens can set "Study Hours" (Study Mode default-on). TechCrunch + Fortune (above).
- Break nudge after 90 min active use within 3-hour window — CNBC Aug 18 (SINGLE-SOURCE).
- Parental controls (linked): Quiet Hours (multiple blocks), disable voice/memory/image gen, opt teen chats out of training, Study Mode default, limited safety notifications. Parents CANNOT read conversations; teens can't override while linked. OpenAI Help https://help.openai.com/en/articles/12315553-parental-controls-in-chatgpt
- Rollout: global Aug 18 2026, Free + paid personal plans; Australia full availability Sept 8 2026. MacRumors https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/18/openai-announces-chatgpt-for-teens/ . Enterprise/Edu/Team not mentioned (inference from "personal plans" — don't state as explicit exclusion).
- Enrollment: stated age 13–17 at signup OR age-prediction estimates under 18 → auto teen experience. TechCrunch (above).

## Age prediction
- Signals: stated age, account age, time-of-day usage, usage patterns over time. CNBC Jan 20 2026 https://www.cnbc.com/2026/01/20/open-ai-age-prediction-chatgpt.html ; Biometric Update https://www.biometricupdate.com/202601/for-chatgpt-openai-rolls-out-age-inference-system-similar-to-youtubes
