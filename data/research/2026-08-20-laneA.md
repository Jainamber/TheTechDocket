## 3. What concretely is slowing — pretraining, deployment, which models, for how long

- **What's paused, precisely:** Reporting consistently describes this as a pause on **reinforcement learning (RL) training**, not base/pretraining. Forbes: "OpenAI instituted a two-week pause in reinforcement learning training on its latest models intended for deployment while it hardened research environments and expanded monitoring systems" (source: https://www.forbes.com/sites/ashishbhatia/2026/08/19/openai-paused-ai-training-for-two-weeks-heres-what-that-means/). Status: **VERIFIED** (Forbes, listed reputable outlet). **I found no source using the word "pretraining"** — treat any claim that base pretraining specifically was halted as unconfirmed; the specific, sourced term throughout is "frontier RL training" / "frontier RL run."
- **Astra-specific containment:** OpenAI's own blog post states it is pausing internal Astra activities/workloads that don't yet meet strengthened security-control requirements, and has rolled out universal monitoring for risky actions and misalignment across agentic applications of Astra (source: https://openai.com/index/responding-next-frontier-critical-cyber-capabilities/). Status: **VERIFIED primary**.
- **The trigger (Preparedness Framework):** OpenAI "cannot rule out" that Astra has reached the **"Critical"** cybersecurity capability tier of its own Preparedness Framework — the first time any OpenAI model has triggered this tier. Threshold definition per reporting: a model that can "identify and develop functional zero-day exploits of all severity levels in many hardened real-world critical systems without human intervention, or devise and execute end-to-end novel strategies for cyberattacks against hardened targets given only a high-level desired goal" (source: https://mlq.ai/news/openai-says-it-cannot-rule-out-critical-cyber-capabilities-in-unreleased-astra-model/, paraphrasing the primary blog). By contrast, GPT-5.6 Sol and earlier models were assessed at "High," not "Critical." Status: **VERIFIED** (multiple corroborating outlets: Axios https://www.axios.com/2026/08/18/openai-pause-astra-preparedness-framework ; The Register).
- **Which model/timeline is affected:** **Astra** (unreleased next-gen model) — its "largest planned frontier [RL] training run remains on hold" as of the Aug 18–19 reporting, with **no confirmed resumption date** stated anywhere I found (source: https://www.axios.com/2026/08/07/openai-astra-model-delay-cybersecurity-risks ; https://www.forbes.com/sites/ashishbhatia/2026/08/19/openai-paused-ai-training-for-two-weeks-heres-what-that-means/). Status: **VERIFIED**.
- **Duration of the disclosed pause:** The 2-week RL pause is described as already having happened/ended by the time of the Aug 18 disclosure ("recently paused... for a little more than two weeks") (source: https://time.com/article/2026/08/18/openai-slowing-training/, corroborated by https://x.com/alexeheath/status/2089777725385109784). The larger Astra frontier run is separate and remains open-ended/on hold. Status: **VERIFIED** for the 2-week figure; **open-ended, no date given** for the larger run.
- **Deployment vs. training:** No evidence found that any already-shipped/deployed model was pulled or paused. GPT-5.6 Sol is described as already in **limited preview to trusted partners**, with release plans shared with the US government in advance and access initially limited at the government's request (source: https://openai.com/index/previewing-gpt-5-6-sol/, corroborated by kingy.ai-style secondary coverage — treat the government-request detail as secondary-sourced only). Status: GPT-5.6 Sol existing/preview facts **VERIFIED primary**; the "limited at government's request" detail is **SINGLE-SOURCE-adjacent** (only saw it via secondary synthesis, flagging for a second look).
- **One outlier claim to flag and NOT use:** cryptobriefing.com published "Astra training not paused, new models still expected to ship soon" (source: https://cryptobriefing.com/openai-astra-training-pause-altman/). This contradicts the weight of primary + reputable-outlet evidence above. CryptoBriefing is not a listed reputable outlet. Status: **UNCONFIRMED / likely contradicted** — do not use without independent verification.
- **Conditions to resume:** Per Forbes/Axios, resumption is conditioned on hardening research environments, red-teaming, expanding monitoring coverage, and rebuilding "confidence in safety" — not a fixed calendar date (source: https://www.forbes.com/sites/ashishbhatia/2026/08/19/openai-paused-ai-training-for-two-weeks-heres-what-that-means/). Status: **VERIFIED**.
