## 3. Latest Guidelines, Evaluation Methods & Quality Rubrics (Concluded & Integrated)

Before any code change, prompt edit, or operational task begins, it must pass the **4-Point North Star Gate (`AGENTS.md`)**:
1. **Target Lever:** Must map to Lever 1, 2, 3, 4, or a strict measurement/auth Enabler.
2. **Causal Mechanism:** State in 1 sentence *how* this action produces more Weekly Visitors.
3. **Payoff Window:** Immediate (`24–48h`), Short-Term (`7–14d`), or Medium-Term (`30d`).
4. **Blacklist Check:** Reject any cosmetic CSS/font redesign, internal-only tool proliferation, refactoring working code for "elegance," or premature ad-network complexity before traffic thresholds are met.

### B. Research & Source Triangulation Guidelines (Stage 2 Anti-Leakage Contract)
Integrated into `prompts/v1/research_queries.md` and enforced by `engine/ai/ai_research.py` + `resolve_links.py`:
1. **Deep-Link Fidelity Rule:** Never cite a bare domain root (`https://example.com/`) or an expiring `vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/...` URL. Every source URL is resolved immediately to its permanent deep link; unresolvable or homepage-only URLs are quarantined in `*-sources-unresolved.json` and excluded from the citable pool.
2. **3-Tier Anti-Leakage Classification:**
   * **`CONFIRMED_PRIMARY`:** Official announcements, vendor docs, SEC/MCA filings, court orders, regulator/PIB releases, arXiv papers.
   * **`REPORTED_SECONDARY`:** Named investigative reporting (e.g., Bloomberg, Reuters, The Information, FT). Must be attributed in prose (*"according to documents viewed by..."*).
   * **`[UNVERIFIED GAP: <reason>]`:** Single-source rumors or missing data. The writer is strictly forbidden from bridging a gap with guesswork or stating an unverified leak as fact.
3. **Entity-Attribute-Value (EAV) Semantic Gap Method (`skills/superseo-skills/semantic-gap-analysis.md`):**
   * **Commodity EAVs:** Basic facts covered by every competitor—compress into $\le 150$ words near the top.
   * **Differentiator EAVs:** Second-order metrics, historical deltas, primary filing numbers, and India landed-cost math missed by surface-level news rewrites—make these the centerpiece of the H2 sections and comparison table.

### C. Quantitative "India Angle" Guidelines (Stage 3 Math Models)
Integrated into `prompts/v1/_style.md` and `prompts/v1/write_article.md`. Generic filler (*"This will also affect Indian users"*) is forbidden; every global article must compute at least one concrete model:

   $$\text{Estimated India Landed Base} = \text{US MSRP}_{\text{USD}} \times \text{FX}_{\text{USD/INR}} \times (1 + \text{BCD}_{\text{eff}}) \times (1 + \text{GST}_{0.18})$$

   $$\text{Effective INR Cost} = \text{USD Price} \times \text{FX}_{\text{USD/INR}} \times (1 + 0.035_{\text{Forex}}) \times (1 + 0.18_{\text{IGST}})$$

### D. Generative Engine Optimization (GEO / AEO) Guidelines (`skills/superseo-skills/geo-optimization.md` + `skills/marketingskills/ai-seo.md`)
Designed to maximize citation frequency in **ChatGPT Search, Perplexity, Claude Search, and Google AI Overviews**:

2. **H2-Top Extractable Answer Spans (+340% AI Citation Lift):** Because AI RAG pipelines chunk HTML by `<h2>` boundaries, **every `##` (H2) section must open with a self-contained 35–55 word direct answer or dated primary metric** in its first two sentences before expanding.
3. **4-Column Grounded Comparison Tables:** Every article must include a Markdown table with $\ge 4$ explicit columns (e.g., `Dimension / Model | Baseline / US | New / India (INR) | Impact / Source`) so LLM parsers extract structured comparisons verbatim.

5. **30-Day Recency Window for ChatGPT Search:** ChatGPT Search heavily weights `dateModified` within the trailing 30 days. Refreshing top evergreen explainers monthly with real Q4 data + `updated:` + `update_note:` keeps them inside the active citation window.
